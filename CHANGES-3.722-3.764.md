# Below the Abbey: updates since 3.722 (3.723 to 3.764)

All of these are live on GitHub Pages. The itch.io browser build and the Windows app are at 3.758 (3.759 to 3.764 are not pushed to itch yet).

## World map (3.723, 3.730 to 3.733)
- 3.723: the world map scrolls further west.
- 3.730: the little cave drawings of the desert dungeons and Sand Road burrows are no longer drawn on the world map (the dungeons themselves are untouched).
- 3.731 to 3.733: the Narrow Sea coast is now a subtle, low, rounded coast with no inlets and no rivers faked as bays; the corners are rounded; the Pilgrim's Path and the river mouths are intact. The volcano is smaller.

## The Pale Spire and the castle (3.724 to 3.729)
- 3.724: no statue shows through the Pilgrim Steps on the Pale Approach.
- 3.725: hanging red lanterns hang from the ceiling above the party; loose ones outdoors are removed.
- 3.726: the Court of Statues stream has rails along its bridges, so the way across the middle is open.
- 3.727: rain is plain rain: no puddles, reflections, wet sheen, lamp smears or ground ripples.
- 3.728: one thin line of splashing puddles at the front of the ground in the rain.
- 3.729: the castle on the horizon of the Pale Approach and the Crooked Road stands still.

## The second floor of the Pale Spire (3.734, 3.739 to 3.742)
- 3.734: the Hall of Illusions, the Dreamkeeper's quest (The Sleeper's Pieces), seven illusion rooms (perfect forest, island by the sea, cabin in the snow, waterways like Venice, desert, swamp, nightmare), seven puzzles, seven body parts (hand, foot, face, chest, legs, heart, brain), the Sleeper's bier and a big reward.
- 3.739 to 3.741: three polish rounds with separate critics: baroque Waterways (the palace, the Bridge of Sighs, a gondola ferry), painted edges and floors in every room, soft floor borders, shade pools under trees, light pools under lamps, floor decals, a colour grade for each room, tree variety, a cabin interior, wrong stairs in the nightmare, seven painted world doors, and an area map for each of the eight levels.
- The Dreamkeeper is a painted old man standing in front of the arrival, and the party's cutscene plays before he speaks.
- 3.742: Waterways bridges keep their size when you walk past or ride under them, the moray eels are replaced by masked cutthroats, the hub has twelve painted wall panels and nine painted doors, the island door glare is fixed, and the Sleeper's bed is a stone box seen right from every side.

## Cutscenes and difficulty (3.735 to 3.738)
- 3.735: the party talks again: the end of the Orange Spire, the end of the Hollow Grasslands, the start and end of the Deeper Hollow maze, the start of the Blighted Lands, the arrival at Sale, the Pale Spire and the Never-Ending Tower, plus seven light ones in unexpected places.
- 3.736 to 3.738: Intelligent adjustment. After four falls in a row (once per play session) the game offers help: easier, weaker enemies for 15 minutes, or a 15 minute +5 boost. Fast winning raises the difficulty a step with the message "master player detected". Switch it off in Menu > Gameplay, which explains it.

## Early game (3.743 to 3.750, 3.762, 3.764)
- 3.743: painted gritty walls and floors, grime decals, torch and lamp light pools and shade in the crypts, the early dungeons, Thornwick, Ironhill and the woods; a few sparse palms in the deserts (the 3D dunes untouched).
- 3.744: the starter crypts at 8 out of 10; four secret rooms with healing potions; Under the Well keeps its blue; the Ironhill Tunnel is sandy tan.
- 3.746 to 3.750: new painted wall torches, then the original torch is back (iron plate and a stick that sticks out) with a slim painted flame; secret walls open on any forward input and are easier to spot; Thornwick and Ironhill exteriors with painted weathered house fronts, a clear muddy path with grass, and mining props in Ironhill.
- 3.762: the pink and mossy wall-foot pieces are gone from the early dungeons.
- 3.764: the torch flame keeps growing as you walk up to it.

## Secret walls, fog of war and potions (3.754)
- Secret walls in 54 mazes and big dungeons (102 rooms, 23.5 percent with a monster): each has a chest of health and mana potions scaled to the dungeon, gold sometimes, and a surprise item. The walls are faint but findable.
- Fog of war on every maze and big dungeon map (Under the Well included).
- Six new mana potion tiers (100 up to 700 mana, seen from level 20 to 140).

## Thornwick (3.753, 3.756, 3.758, 3.759)
- Windows in Thornwick and Ironhill light up from dusk to dawn.
- Roofs on every Thornwick house, seated on the walls with chimneys and a church spire. They were taken out in 3.755 while being fixed and returned in 3.756 and 3.758. The sliding up close is still being worked on.
- The real estate board is on the edge of the road, not in the middle (3.759).

## Characters and quests
- Tiki, the Lost Cat: a skinny white cat with one blue and one gold eye replaces the tabby under the Thornwick well (3.753).
- The travelling bard walks through, is normal height, and leaves after her song; no person added later can block a route (3.754).

## Gandrick's cave (3.757, 3.760, 3.763)
- A tunnel maze with six big halls (more than 960 open squares), Gandrick in the far hall behind the iron gate, eight extra bandits in the tunnels, four secret rooms, and barrels of stolen goods in the corners of the halls. No brazier stands in front of a door (3.760). A teleporter stone stands in Gandrick's hall (3.763).

## Weather (3.751, 3.753)
- Woods, roads and towns in the north use the layered Blighted Road rain with sound. Each outdoor area visit has a 15 percent chance of rain (not in deserts or indoors). The Blighted Lands and Sale rain about half the time.

## Interface and sound (3.745, 3.747, 3.753, 3.761)
- The first dungeon's music plays in the towns (not Khemetra).
- After resting the night, the game returns to the play screen.
- Area doors sound once; Take all is a bigger button with the sort buttons under it.
- The default look is light scanlines, no dithering and no sharpening (saved graphics settings reset once).
