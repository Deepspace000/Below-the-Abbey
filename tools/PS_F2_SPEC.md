# Pale Spire, level 2: the Hall of Illusions and the seven illusion rooms (spec)

Owner's brief, verbatim: "Start level 2 of the pale spire. Level 2 will be a main area in the same style as level 1, except smaller, and it will have a quest giver at the start. The quest giver will introduce the room as the place of illusions. 7 rooms link off it. Each room opens into a different world. Room 1, a perfect forest, room 2, a beautiful island by the sea. Room 3, a cabin in a snowy land. Room 4, a waterway, like Venice. Room 5, a desert, room 6, a swamp, room 7, a nightmare. An item needs to be retrieved from, each room, a hand, a foot, a face, a chest, a pair of legs, a heart, and a brain. The 7 items then need to be assembled. Each level is moderate size and has enemies from the illusion world. The item is hidden in each level behind a fairly simple puzzle."

Standards: all of CLAUDE.md and tools/PS_COHERENCE_BRIEF.md and tools/GAME_FIX_BRIEF.md apply. Painted raster art only (Gemini via Claude in Chrome; reuse existing painted pieces first), strict front elevation, nothing floating, nothing on routes or in one-wide passages, wall pictures on real walls, NPCs normal height (about 1.0), enemies fully visible in fights, no em dashes, signs show the place name only, no loose arches or doors, plain literal dialogue (no cryptic or poetic text).
Done means: `python tools/ps_audit.py --ground` prints AUDIT CLEAN; ps_test load, links, reach, walk, key, quests, save, dest, dash; nt_test build; pg_test pass; `python tools/game_audit.py --levels 9820-9827` shows nothing to fix; and your own end-to-end puzzle test passes.

Bring all seven to the Sleeper's table (a central dais or bier in the hub) and assemble them: the Sleeper wakes. The assembly: the table shows which parts are placed (7 slots; placing a part consumes the item and sets F.placed[part]); a visible assembled figure (painted front-elevation sprite of a pale figure on a bier: Gemini; the parts may appear progressively as decor pictures) once all seven are placed.

## The seven puzzles (fairly simple; each uses existing machinery: clickable decor or NPC (khNpc, say()), items, flags; never soft-lockable; a wrong answer resets and may release a few themed foes; state resets sensibly if the party leaves)
1. Perfect Forest, the Hand: a glade ring of four mossy standing stones carved with the four seasons (a bud, a sun, a falling leaf, a snowflake). A hollow oak in the centre holds the hand behind a bark door. A carved plaque (plain text) says the stones must be touched in the order of the year. Touch them in the order spring (bud), summer (sun), autumn (leaf), winter (snowflake); a wrong touch resets with a soft chime. Foes: wolves, thorn stags, treants, wasp swarms (the perfect forest still bites).
2. Island by the Sea, the Foot: a message in a bottle on the shore gives the clue ("dig where the tide leaves the first footprint of the day"). Four sand patches carry different shell marks (conch, scallop, starfish, spiral); a spade is picked up from the fisherman's hut (a small fetch step); only the patch whose shell matches the bottle's drawing holds the foot. A wrong dig releases crabs. Foes: giant crabs, sirens, gulls, sea serpent spawn.
3. Cabin in the Snow, the Face: the cabin hearth is cold: three logs lie in the snow around the cabin (small floor pickups) and flint lies on a post; put the logs on the hearth inside and strike the flint; the fire melts the ice on the mirror over the mantel, revealing the face. Foes: wolves, frost wraiths, snow bears, an ice elemental.
4. Waterways (Venice-like), the Chest: canals, bridges and gondolas; a locked palazzo door with three carnival masks (plague doctor, harlequin, moon) hung beside it; a plaque says the Doge admits only the one who watches and says nothing (plain clue: the owl-eyed plague doctor mask). Pick the mask for the door; a wrong mask releases eels from the canal. Foes: eels, drowned gondoliers, water sprites, masked cutthroats.
5. Desert, the Legs: a buried temple courtyard whose floor is eight tiles (scarab, eye, ankh, sun) in a 2 by 4 grid; a wall frieze (plain description) shows a sequence of four symbols to step on in order (floor plates, real pressure squares), which opens the sealed sarcophagus door that holds the legs; a wrong step spills sand scorpions. Foes: scorpions, mummies, sand wraiths, dust devils.
6. Swamp, the Heart: a witch's hut and cauldron: a plain note lists three things to put in the cauldron: a toadstool, an eel and a clump of marsh moss (each found at a small marked spot in the swamp, guarded by foes); put all three in and the heart rises from the pot. Foes: leeches, bog hags, will-o-wisps, swamp trolls, giant frogs.
7. Nightmare, the Brain: a world of staring doors and wrong stairs; a memory trial: a ring of four lanterns lights in a sequence (four steps, shown clearly) and the player touches them back in the same order; fail and shadows come, then it replays; success opens the jar holding the brain. Foes: shadows, many-eyed horrors, nightmare hounds, drowned dolls (use the Hollow and Blighted horrors).
Each room: 48 x 40, moderate: an arrival glade (or shore, street, dune), the puzzle area at the far north end, 2 or 3 clearings with foes (about 10 to 16 foes total, level 103 or 104, kept off the main route and out of the arrival area), a few chests with gold and potions, ambient sound and sky fitting the biome, and a safe arrival square. Each world must look unmistakably different from the castle (real painted biome art) but with a seam that says illusion: the border of each illusion is a pale silver mist wall with cracks of black stone showing through. Fog of war (cloud cover on the map, ps_fog.js) applies.
