Other builders append their own sections below; re-read before writing.

## Owner references (tools/PALE_SPIRE_REFERENCES.md, pictures tools/ref_spire/ref8 to ref14)
What package 6 takes from them (walls, doors, floors, silhouette, Gemini prompts):
* Walls: pale ashlar, and an `ivy` wall style (pale stone hung with dark cold ivy, in the kit as style 'ivy') for cloisters, the courtyard facade and arcades. Pointed arches, carved windows with tracery (style 'win'), balustrades with urns.
* Floors: pale flagstones, wet dark cobbles (ref14, floor 'cobble' and 'wet'), gravel, clipped pale lawn beside paths, black and white marble in halls, violet runner.
* Castle silhouette (for the S9 gate and fronts sheet and the map picture): many conical dark-slate towers of different heights with banners, steep gables, tiers of terraces, a lower bastion with three arched tunnel gateways and stairs climbing both sides to the terrace (ref9); a wide stepped approach to an arched double door under a carved rose window (ref8).
* Gate mood (ref11): iron gate at the end of a cobbled path between low parapets, roses climbing the walls, glowing windows, storm sky; in the Pale Spire pale silver and violet, red only as accent.
* Nooks (ref13, ref14): statues on plinths in each arch, mossy benches, stream into a lily pond, curved stairs, pale wrong flowers (white, bruise purple, one red).
* Gemini prompt additions for sheet S8: "pale grey ashlar, cold, hung with dark green ivy", "wet dark cobbles with puddles reflecting violet light", "flagstones pale silver grey", always STRICT FRONT ELEVATION, magenta ground.

No edit of maps_world_*.js: ps_maps.js unshifts the interior row and gives the `palespire` WMB_PLACES entry a live `ids` getter (built ids only, so the padlock goes once a level is built).
Hooks: `pspOnArrive(fn)` for builders; `pspKeyBump(link)` is called (if defined) when a locked stair door is bumped without the key (psq10 offer).
Painted art: none yet. All pictures are stand-ins (`PSP_PICS`, pale filtered copies of existing pieces); drop painted pieces in `assets/ps/` with `assets/ps/manifest.json` {name:{file,h}} and they replace the stand-ins with no code change. Test: `python tools/ps_test.py <mode>` on your own server (PS_PORT, BTA_CHROME). Modes load links reach key quests walk save dash shots. Page loading the art takes 10 to 15 s, tests wait for PSP.ready.
Flags: `G.flags.psp` (pspF()): `last` (last level reached), `seenNNNN`, builders add their own (key2, sacristyOpen, framePainted, sovereignLore ...).

## Package 5: the dungeons, rebuilt compact and vertical (30 Oct 2026)
File: `src/desert/ps_dungeon.js` (prefix psd / PSD). Levels 9780 (48 x 40), 9790 (48 x 40), 9800 (40 x 36); all link squares and landings of ps_ids.js are unchanged and tested.
* Own maker `psdMake` (not pspMake): the dungeons need water floors, parapets, secret doors and cell gates. The maps are drawn with rectangles in `PSD_PLAN[id]` (legend at the top of that block); furnishing is in `PSD.SPEC[id].dress`.
* Look: black stone (texBlack) tinted green, wall height 3.8 (Gaols), 4.6 (Deep Cells), 5.2 (Oubliette); verdigris streaks, moss, roots and ivy, slit windows (pale green light shaft), blind pointed arcades, pier blocks, carved faces (a ring of skulls round the Hunger's pit, a block of faces in the Question Room), all made in code as wall tiles with the detail in the lower 2.7 units (what the party can see). Sickly green-white lights (`lcol`), green wall flames: `psdFlames()` adds a sixth flame set (torch spec `fl:5`); no warm light anywhere in the dungeons.
* Vertical: tall vaulted walls, a winding stair (THE CHAIN STAIR, two warp doors, Chain Room (7,13) to the south passage (4,27)), a stone cross bridge with parapet posts over the dark cistern, columns standing in the water, three wheel platforms, a winding way down to the Oubliette ring, a pit surrounded by faces.
* Secrets: the service crawl behind the north cells of the Gaols (secret door at (21,15), bump to open, a chest and a statue in an alcove at its end); the bone chamber behind the east wall of the Oubliette (32,29). Opening is kept in `G.flags.psp.d.sec`. The doors carry `psw:'secret'` as well so that tools/ps_test.py reach counts them as openable (ps_dungeon is outermost in the khBump chain, so ps_wings never sees them).
* Statue alcoves: single open squares in the walls with a blocking statue (st_veiled, st_hand, st_mourners, st_bishop, st_headless).
* All gameplay kept: ten cells, nine prisoners and their talk, psq30 to psq38 with hand-ins, the tin chest, five Name Stones, the wheel order puzzle (SOUTH, WEST, EAST), the Drowned Gaoler (rises on the bridge), the Questioner, the Hunger, the 9,000 gold chest, the flooded half drains after the wheels.
* Tested on port 8615 with a copy of ps_test.py that boots with ?town=palespire6 (the stale package 1 file in this worktree cannot build 9720): load, reach, walk, save, dash and quests pass for 9780, 9790, 9800. Links to 9750 report the missing other packages only.
* For the art agent (stand-ins show until painted): st_veiled, st_hand, st_mourners, st_bishop, st_headless (creepy statues for alcoves), cell_door, valve_wheel (now a rune stone), rack, iron_maiden, stocks, chains, straw_bed, shackled_skeleton, cage, brazier; painted wall textures to replace the code-drawn black stone, arcade and face tiles; a painted green flame.
* Weak: no real floor heights (stairs are warps), alcove statues are stand-ins, cells are all 3 x 3, creatures are stand-ins, the ceiling is one plain texture.
