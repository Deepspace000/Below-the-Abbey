Other builders append their own sections below; re-read before writing.

## Owner references (tools/PALE_SPIRE_REFERENCES.md, pictures tools/ref_spire/ref8 to ref14)
What package 6 takes from them (walls, doors, floors, silhouette, Gemini prompts):
* Walls: pale ashlar, and an `ivy` wall style (pale stone hung with dark cold ivy, in the kit as style 'ivy') for cloisters, the courtyard facade and arcades. Pointed arches, carved windows with tracery (style 'win'), balustrades with urns.
* Floors: pale flagstones, wet dark cobbles (ref14, floor 'cobble' and 'wet'), gravel, clipped pale lawn beside paths, black and white marble in halls, violet runner.
* Castle silhouette (for the S9 gate and fronts sheet and the map picture): many conical dark-slate towers of different heights with banners, steep gables, tiers of terraces, a lower bastion with three arched tunnel gateways and stairs climbing both sides to the terrace (ref9); a wide stepped approach to an arched double door under a carved rose window (ref8).
* Gate mood (ref11): iron gate at the end of a cobbled path between low parapets, roses climbing the walls, glowing windows, storm sky; in the Pale Spire pale silver and violet, red only as accent.
* Nooks (ref13, ref14): statues on plinths in each arch, mossy benches, stream into a lily pond, curved stairs, pale wrong flowers (white, bruise purple, one red).
* Gemini prompt additions for sheet S8: "pale grey ashlar, cold, hung with dark green ivy", "wet dark cobbles with puddles reflecting violet light", "flagstones pale silver grey", always STRICT FRONT ELEVATION, magenta ground.

## Package 1 (redesign, 30 Oct 2026): Sale's west gate, the Approach, the Gatehouse, the Winch Loft, the Porter's Lodge, and the shared kit
Files: `src/desert/ps_gate.js` (rebuilt), `src/desert/ps_kit.js` (palette, sky, helpers), a 7 line edit in `src/desert/sale_town.js` (west gate), one token in `blight_sky.js` (9720 no longer uses the road's storm sky), small edits in `ps_core.js` (stand-in toning follows PSP_PAL) and `tools/ps_test.py` (PS_ONLY env, pits and ravines are not "cut off", ext links have no twin).
* PALETTE: ONE table, `PSP_PAL` at the top of ps_kit.js (moonlit teal-blue night, black and charcoal stone, brass and aged-orange banners, verdigris only as small accents). Wall styles, doors, floors, ceilings, sky, mist and the stand-in toning all read it. To re-tone other packages, edit the table (keys: stone stoneGain copper* verdigris bone brass banner moss ivy glass silver violet green warm amb sky mist moon floorTint). `C.silver C.violet C.green C.warmCol` are the same colours on the kit. Lights outdoors and indoors are cold teal; warm only in rest rooms.
* SKY: every outdoor castle level gets the kit sky automatically (night, stars, thin cloud, mist; the engine reads it through BKSKY_PAN; no other code needed). A crescent moon (`moon_crescent`) and two bat flocks (`bat_flock`) are drawn into the sky each frame; `tex_sky_night` is registered for the art agent (not read yet). The CASTLE VIEW: `pspMake({castle:{at:[x,y],far,near,lift,lift1,h0,h1}})` draws `castle_view` (far) and `castle_view_near` (swapped in by distance) into the sky at the world point `at`; the picture grows from h0 to h1 sky rows and sinks from `lift` to `lift1` rows above the horizon as the party climbs. The sky shows only about 105 rows above the horizon (about 20 degrees): paint the castle wide and tall (stand-in aspect 3.2) with a misty foot; width on screen is rows x aspect, pieces are drawn at 0.57 width in the sky strip (done for you). The Approach uses at (32,-40), far 93 (foot of the climb), near 49 (barbican). Until painted, ps_gate.js draws a spire skyline stand-in (psgDrawCastle) and replaces it automatically.
* REAL HEIGHTS: `pspMake({terrace:true})` (outdoor only) turns the level into a terraced level of the city engine: ground height per cell from `C.hgt` (C.rise, C.ravine, C.bridge set it), walls stand on it, the party really climbs and crosses bridges over drops. The kit also switches off the Hollow Gold city sky objects for that level (HGTG.done). Cells that are open to the eye but closed to the feet (pits, ravines, fence cells) are `block[]=1` with `rows '.'`; decor on them must be `block:false`.
* Helpers (signatures in the ps_kit.js comment): C.rise C.ravine C.bridge C.fence C.vines C.vineWall C.archway C.alcove, C.prop(name,x,y,{maxW}) (maxW keeps stand-in fences narrow), pspMake({walled:true}).
* New picture names this package asks for (registered in ps_gate.js and ps_kit.js, stand-ins until painted): gate_clock (5.0 cells tall, the clock-tower gate arch front), banner_long_a, banner_long_b (3.2), buttress_a, buttress_b (3.6), moon_crescent, bat_flock, tex_sky_night. Existing S10 names used: arch_*, alcove_*, vine_*, iron_fence_*, bridge_rail, clock_face, window_*, rose_pale, urn_big, statue_plinth, well_pale, cart_old, lantern_bracket.
* Approach 9720 (64 x 56): five switchback legs (y 51, 44, 37, 30, 23), ground rising 0.3 to 3.9, joined by stair passages; between legs a retaining wall, three pit rows and an iron fence (see over to the leg below); a gully bridge on leg 3; three hidden nooks with a chest and statue each; the cairn niche (psq1); the Long Bridge over the ravine at x32; the Barbican forecourt (bays behind iron fences) and the Great Gate at (32,6) under a round clock face and two long banners. Sale link: tile (32,54) to Sale (4,36,1).
* Gatehouse 9721 (32 x 24): passage x15..17 with arches and the Gate Captain under the raised gate, west and east guardrooms, a gate chapel and the gaoler's closet (side chambers). Winch Loft 9722 (20 x 18): stair hall, murder-hole grating walk, winch chamber, the Warden's table with the horn, a gallery. Lodge 9723 (18 x 14): rest room with the porter.
* Test notes: `PS_ONLY=9720,9721,9722,9723 python tools/ps_test.py <mode>` limits a mode to those levels (modes load links reach walk save dash pass for them).
