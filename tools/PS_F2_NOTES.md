Brief: tools/PS_F2_SPEC.md. Built 31 Oct 2026 by one agent. Jump links: `?town=palespire9` (the hub), `?town=illusion1` to `?town=illusion7` (the rooms; passed on as `palespire21` to `palespire27`, see below).

## The hub (9820, 56 x 44, indoor)
Built with pspMake (indoor) and then re-walled with the Great Halls' own tiles (`pshTile`, `pshFloors`, ceiling `psc_hall`), so it matches level 1. Stair Landing (arrival 28,38; the Dreamkeeper at 26,35, in view from the arrival), the nave (columns, stained windows, portraits) with the Sleeper's table on a dais at 28,22, the Gallery of Doors (y 4 to 6: five doors, a name plaque and one candelabrum by each), the West and East Arms (column aisles, alcoves, mirrors; swamp and nightmare doors), four side rooms (Room of Mirrors, Library of Dreams, Room of Masks, Sleepers' Beds), the Quiet Chapel (rest, `pspRest`) and the Gallery of the Sleeper (her story on a lectern). No creatures (safe rect). The iron door at 52,5 is the third floor stair: it says it is shut (KH_DOOR `psf2:'stair3'`).
Quest psq40 "The Sleeper's Pieces" (giver and hand-in: the Dreamkeeper). Step 1: find the seven pieces. Step 2: lay them on the table (the table's talk takes every piece in the bag), then "Wake her": the woken Sleeper (`psf2_sleeper`, painted standing figure) appears beside the table and speaks. Reward (doubled by QREW_MUL): 30,000 gold, 180,000 XP, The Sleeper's Circlet (`pg_dreamcrown`, head, in PGR.PLACE), two elixirs and a super mana potion. `psf2F().done` is set on hand-in. The Dreamkeeper gives a plain hint per room ("Tell me about one of the doors").

## The room maker (f2Room)
48 x 40, outdoor kind, arrival 24,35 north, the way back at 24,38 set in a patch of black castle stone with two lantern pillars and a plaque (THE HALL OF ILLUSIONS). The outer ring is the MIST WALL (painted texture `f2_tex_mist`, pale silver mist with cracks of black stone). spec: `routes` (centre lines, `rw` half width, per route `{p,rw}`), `clear` (clearings: rect or ellipse, floor, map name), `band` (open ground beyond the core) or `noBand`, `edge(C,x,y)` (what is beyond the band: water, walls; default mist). Helpers on C: `fill` (blocking filler in the band, denser further out), `scrub`, `capTrees` (a canopy next to a walkable square is capped and pushed back), `spot`/`foes` (creatures off the paths and 7 or more from the arrival), `chestAt`, `plaque` (lettered slab), `warp`, `ret` (the way back), `done` (no sealed pockets, no blocking prop in a one-wide gap, lights, fog, and a last reach check on the finished level that turns any unreachable square into mist wall).
The castle passes (ps_curate, ps_tidy, ps_doors, ps_scale ...) wrap KH_LAZY at their own load time, before these files register, so they do not run on the second floor; the hub and rooms call `pswaHang` themselves and arrange everything by hand. Signs, flush and fog (render-time passes) apply as on every castle level.
Sky: each room has its own painted-in-code panorama (`f2SkyArr`, biome colours, soft cloud) instead of the castle's night sky (`pspSkyFrame` is wrapped). Weather: snow falls in 9823, heat shimmer in 9825 (`weatherNow` fx). Floors: existing engine floors (lush, trail, sand, bluewater, pale cobble, canal water, mud, planks, tombdark, chequer), plus re-toned ones (`F2_FLMAKE`: snow, bog water, blood sand) and the four carved desert plates from Gemini.

## The seven puzzles (state in `psf2F().rooms[n]`, saved; half-done sequences reset on arrival)
1. Forest 9821 (hand): four standing stones (bud, sun, leaf, snowflake) round the hollow oak, not in year order round the ring. Touch spring, summer, autumn, winter; a wrong touch resets with a low chime. The bark door opens; take the hand from the oak.
2. Island 9822 (foot): the bottle on the south shore says dig where the tide leaves the first footprint of the day, with a drawing of a starfish. The spade leans by the fisherman's hut (east). Four sand mounds on the north beach (conch, scallop, starfish, spiral); without the spade there is no Dig option. A wrong dig floods the hole and releases two giant crabs; the starfish mound holds the foot.
3. Snow 9823 (face): three logs in the snow (west by the frozen pond, east by the bear's den, south-west in the birches), flint and steel on the post by the woodpile. The cabin door (24,8) leads into the room inside (built walled in mist in the north-east corner, 40..43 x 4..7, door out at 41,8). Lay the logs, strike the flint: the fire burns, the ice on the mirror melts, take the face.
4. Waterways 9824 (chest): the Doge's palazzo door (24,2) with three masks on hooks (harlequin, plague doctor, moon) and the plaque THE DOGE ADMITS ONLY THE ONE WHO WATCHES AND SAYS NOTHING. The plague doctor (owl eyes, a beak that cannot open) opens the door; a wrong mask brings out two masked cutthroats (no eels: the owner found them wrong; they were replaced by land creatures). Bump the open door for the chest.
5. Desert 9825 (legs): the sunken temple court; the frieze on the north wall shows scarab, eye, ankh, sun (a slab by the way in says so in words). Eight plates in two rows of four (two of each symbol); step on them in the frieze order (`F2.step`). A wrong plate pours out two scorpions and resets. The sarcophagus opens; take the legs.
6. Swamp 9826 (heart): the witch's note (post by the cauldron) asks for a toadstool, an eel and a clump of marsh moss, each at a place marked with a red rag (west, east, south-west). Put all three in the cauldron; the heart rises. The witch's hut door is barred.
7. Nightmare 9827 (brain): four lanterns in a ring round the jar. Ask the jar to show the order: the lanterns flare red one after another, each with a message (north-west, north-east, south-east, south-west). Touch them back in the same order; a wrong touch brings two shadows, a new order is drawn and shown again. The clasps open; take the brain. The walls of the nightmare carry staring doors that never open (KH_DOOR `psf2:'nightdoor'`).
