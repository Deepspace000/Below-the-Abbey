# Pale Spire indoor critic (levels 9740 9750 9760 9780 9790 9800)

Method: tools/polish/psi_walk.py teleports the party to spread or fixed viewpoints and shoots them; every picture was looked at.
Rounds: r1 (before), r2 to r4 (halls after ghosts, textures, haunt dresser), r5 to r6 (wings, dungeon), r7 to r8 (stair hall, wings re-tint).

## Ranked findings (worst first) and what was done

1. Folk sprites scaled up as ghosts and creatures (a giant lute-playing minstrel in the music salon and the nave, Oldhaven folk as prisoners, a jester statue in the banqueting hall). FIXED: all ghosts now use the painted gaunt p_ghost_* pieces (priest, butler, maid, gentleman, lady, child, soldier, dancer), mapped per haunting so neighbours differ; hostile servants, courtiers, dancers and carvers use the same pieces tinted, so a foe is not a harmless ghost; the nine prisoners wear painted ghost faces; hall statues use painted st_* pieces; no kh_ohfolk or kh_ohsold left in the three files.
2. The key hunt was a sacristy puzzle only. FIXED: the Second Floor Key lies in a chained strongbox on a dais at the north end of the Oubliette pit (violet glow, candelabra, chains), opened only after the Hunger is dead. Sacristy keeps its candle puzzle and Sacristan boss but now pays a journal, 6,000 gold and a potion. Graded clues (pspClue 1 to 3): locked stair doors ("not on this floor, look below"), Steward ledger, key hook cabinet (chalked BELOW), library book, ghost whisper in the stair hall, marble stair ghosts, scratched message in a Deep Cells cell, gaoler log in the Key Room, Ansel Peck, Hob Garrick, sacristy journal. psq10 is the explicit key quest with four journal steps that follow the clue level.
3. Wings and dungeons read flat blue or flat green. PARTLY FIXED: wing walls switched from grey to the black painted stone with a cold tint. Dungeon green is on purpose but heavy; the walls still need painted ashlar, moss and vine textures per room.
4. The nave had no clerestory, no cathedral feel (ref16). FIXED partly: eight tall stained windows high in both walls, painted vault ceiling (tex_vault_ceiling) and painted black marble and flagstone (tex_*) replace the code textures. Still missing: ribbed vault springing from the columns, pews seen as a long rows (pew sprites are low stand-ins), a lit altarpiece at the far end.
5. Grand Stair: flat plain door. FIXED: two mossy flights, a barred iron gate prop, chains, an arch, vines, banners and a cold light before the door. Still weak: no real height steps (indoor levels have no floor height mechanism), the double door itself is the shared door tile.
6. Haunted house dressing. A shared dresser (pshHaunt in ps_halls.js, used by halls, wings and dungeon) places haunted mirrors (talkable), ancestor portraits (some talkable), sheeted windows, corner webs, thick webs in one-wide passages, candles with warm light, red lanterns with red light, dust-sheeted furniture and spiders. Mirrors, candles and portraits show stand-ins now; the rest appear only when the painted pieces exist (webs, sheets, red lanterns, dust sheets), so they cannot look wrong in the meantime.
7. Too many large figures at close range (the priest ghost fills the view). Ghost heights come from the painted manifest (1.8); consider 1.6.
8. Dark rooms in 9760 stand blank when the view faces a wall: needs a light pool and a wall picture on every long blank wall (the dresser adds some).
9. Creature art still not gaunt: gyi_zombie style stand-ins for pscook, psmonk, psdrowned, pscellghost and the pale zombie in the gaols; the art agent should repaint the list in PSP_CREATURES.

Wanted painted: p_ghost_monk, p_ghost_coachman, p_ghost_hunter, p_ghost_choir (children), gaunt servant and courtier foes, and all haunted names (window_sheeted_a to c, cobweb_*, mirror_haunted_a to c, candles_glow_a to c, lantern_red_a and b, portrait_ancestor_1 to 8, dust_sheet_furniture_a and b, web_spider, bed_cobweb_a and b).
