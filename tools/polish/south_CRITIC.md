# Critic review: Deeper Hollow polish B (precincts 13 to 24, atrium, gate)

Reviewer method: own server on port 8381, `?town=deephollow&lvl=34` (level 475), 80 full size screenshots (1100x760, software GL) taken by teleporting the party: 12 vignettes, 4 signposts, 2 lamps, 4 atrium positions, 11 quest givers, 4 shops, church, villagers, 6 dead ends, 4 long climbs, 4 junctions, 4 plaque positions. Each one looked at with the Read tool. Screenshots are in the scratchpad `cb/A`, `cb/B`, `cb/C` (names below). The agent's audit (`b_audit.js`) was re-run: 25,588 reachable cells, 2,757 zone decor pieces, 0 wall decor, 0 empty note texts, 38 distinct notes, 0 duplicate notes, 0 em dashes, no unreachable people. My rough "blockCorr" metric lists chests in 2-neighbour cells (about 40); the agent's exact flood fill says none cut a one-square way, and I walked past none of them in the shots, so I accept 0.

## Defects

1. **Statues, plinths and lamps tower over the corridor (HIGH, proportions).** `hgtb_deco_shellstatue` 2.0 high (14 of them), `hgtb_deco_pearlplinth` 1.8 (13), `art_hgxstat*` 1.25 to 1.31 (about 70), `hg_lamp` 2.2 (45): the audit table in `b_audit.md`. Villager is 0.9 and walls are about 1.0, so these fill or break the top of the view. Repro: the owl statue `v_cache` at 129,55 facing 2 hides half the corridor; `lamp_35_71` at 35,71 facing 0 is cut off by the top edge; shell statue at 179,45 (de4 shot, from 176,45 facing 1) is as tall as the corridor. Fix: cap decor statues at 1.0 to 1.1, plinths at 1.0, lamps at 1.4 (keep landmark lamps at 1.6 in rooms only).
2. **Shop signs and hanging boards are bigger than the shopkeepers (MEDIUM).** `sign_store`, `sign_smith`, `sign_inn`, `sign_church`, `hgdpc_sg_armour`, `hgdpc_sg_magic` are 1.05 to 1.09 high against 0.95 to 1.0 frogs. Repro: Tessera shop at 121,12 facing 2 (shop6): pouch sign covers a third of the screen; Dunewell at 42,86 (q4) the DUNEWELL board is bigger than the quest frog. Fix: 0.6 to 0.7 high.
3. **Wizard shopkeeper dwarfs his stall; bookcases bigger than people (MEDIUM).** `hg_wizard` is 1.0 but his awning reads about 1.5 times his height (shop7 at 141,27 facing 2). `wm_bookshelf` 1.71 and `hgtb_furn_bookshelf` 1.6 beside a 0.9 villager (q11 at 14,66 facing 2: bookcase twice the lectern). Fix: bookcases to 1.2 to 1.3.
4. **House art is out of scale (MEDIUM).** `hg_house0` 3.1 and `hg_house1` 2.5 high next to 0.9 villagers (thf0 at 63,75 and beekeeper at 91,65 facing 1). Reads like a tower. Fix: 1.8 to 2.0 or accept by design and say so in the notes.
5. **The 26 signposts and 12 vignette notes are the same handprint paper (HIGH, signage and repetition).** `hgdb_post` and `hgdb_note` both use `hl_p_note` (hgd_polish_b.js lines 30 and 31, 99, 120, 132), 126 pieces in the level. On a signpost the player sees a scrap with a red handprint (post_132_75, post_33_65, post_185_84 from the cell facing the wall), never a name or arrow. The directions you computed are only visible after clicking. Fix: use the existing signpost art (`wm_signpost` or the `sign_*` boards, 0.7 high) for `hgdb_post`, and a chalk or carved variant for notes; keep the handprint to one grim vignette.
6. **Blue Quarter and Mosaic Halls are near black (HIGH, lighting).** Repro: `v_tile` at 126,3 facing 3; `v_drywell` at 12,81 facing 1; church5 at 36,29 facing 2 (cross sign barely visible); `fwcook` at 28,29 facing 2 (two frogs, no light on them); de1 at 5,4 facing 0 and de5 at 21,62 facing 2 are black rectangles; long4 at 71,49 facing 3. Vignette lights do not light the vignette. Fix: ambient floor for biomes 5 and 6, or add a light every 8 squares along long runs and at each dead end.
7. **Two placement problems at quest givers (MEDIUM).** `hgd_q9` (75,25): a lamp stands directly between camera and the giver (shot q9, from 75,21 facing 2), the giver is a tiny frog behind a lamp post. `hgd_hermit` (148,71): a mantis-type creature occupies the neighbouring cell and fills the entire view (hermit shot from 152,71 facing 3). Fix: move the lamp one cell, and keep creatures at least 3 squares from named quest givers.
8. **Long climbs are empty (MEDIUM, navigation).** 62, 78, 78 and 60 squares of straight corridor with only a lamp at the far end (long4, long5, long6, long7). Not done by the agent (admitted). Fix: a bench, signpost and light every 15 squares, plus a side niche; consider shortcuts only after a save test.
9. **Dead ends are still dull (MEDIUM).** de1 (5,4), de3 (6,37 has a large chest, good), de5 (21,62): the view down the corridor shows nothing before the end, items are within 2 squares but off axis or in darkness. Fix: put the object on the corridor axis one square from the end and light it.
10. **Vignette props repeat (MEDIUM).** Barrel, crates and candle recur in Cold Camp, Dry Well, Barricade and Waiting Bench; no bedroll, fire or ladder (agent's own "not done"). Banner at Trophy Wall (171,11 facing 3) is a different style from the surrounding gold, and the antlers lie on the floor, not on a wall. Tile Counter (126,3) shows a dark open book, a few petals and nothing a tiler would own.
11. **Tall spiralfern, bulb flowers and shell flowers (LOW).** `hgt_bulbflower` 1.9, `hgt_shellflower` 2.1, `hgtb_plant_spiralfern` 1.6, `hgt_vine_roundleaf` up to 3.47. In the atrium the spiral fern (gate shot plaque_c at 100,6) is taller than the gate at 0.95 (`gate` entry in the table). The gate to the Maze of Gardens should be the tallest object near it. Fix: gate to 1.5, fern to 0.9.
12. **Leftover jokes from the other zone (LOW, text).** Notes 1, 11, 16, 17 mention the hedge maze, Hedgefoot and the honeycomb walls inside the sandstone and tile halls. Fix: gate each note to the biome where it is true.
13. **Missing icons (MEDIUM, loading).** 66 files give 404 on every load, 12 are `icn_hgd*` for this zone. Fix: add the PNGs or alias them to existing ones.
14. **Petal cloud still big (LOW).** `hgdfc_pink1` up to 1.68 high and 1.94 aspect (q10 at 172,31 facing 2: a petal bush fills half the room and hides the quest giver at 172,35). Fix: cap at 1.0.

## Verdict

FIX FIRST. The patch is clean on engineering (no blockers, no console errors, 225 ms), but the user's explicit ask was proportions, and statues, lamps, signs and houses are still 1.5 to 3 times a person; and the signposts do not look like signs. Fix defects 1, 2, 5 and 6, then ship.
