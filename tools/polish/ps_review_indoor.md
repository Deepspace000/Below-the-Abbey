# Pale Spire indoor review (9740 9750 9760 9780 9790 9800)

Reviewer: independent art director and level designer, hostile on purpose. Branch review-indoor (from pale-redesign 6c0f9bd0). Nothing in src or assets was edited.
Method: 144 spread screenshots (24 per level, farthest-point sampling, tools/polish/psi_walk.py), about 190 targeted screenshots (landmarks, doors, bosses, gargoyles, cells, pit, key dais; tools/polish/ri_shoot.py), objective level data dumps (tools/polish/ri_data.py, ri_an.py, ri_map.py, ri_reach.py, ri_doors.py, ri_lum.py), and tools/ps_test.py load, key, walk, links (all pass), reach (fails as listed below). Every image was looked at. Key shots: tools/polish/review_indoor/*.jpg (40 files; names starting GOOD are things to keep).

## The three owner questions, plain answers

1. AAA dungeon crawler? Not yet. 9740 and the Oubliette pit come close in single frames (GOOD_* files). The rest reads as a very good indie build with stand-in leftovers: flat generated brick walls with glowing outlines in 9750 and 9760, monochrome green dungeons with one repeated leaf-blob wall, floating gargoyles, billboard stairs, padlock icons for the barred doors. Props and ghosts are painted and strong; walls, floors, ceilings, doors and stairs are the weak layer.
2. Gothic, beautiful and threatening at once? Gothic: yes in 9740, 9760 chapel, bell and clock rooms, the pit. Beautiful: only 9740 (black marble, twisted columns, candelabra, ancestors, ghost couples). Threatening: ghosts, portraits, webs and the Hunger are; but ordinary foes are plain (pale grey man with a club, upright plump rats, kitchen rat heaps) and not "thin withered gaunt evil" as the owner asked.
3. Balanced? Visually inconsistent between levels (each level has its own wall look, three different palettes: black-teal, flat cyan-blue, sickly green). Mechanically flat: nearly every normal foe is "DEADLY, 5 to 8 rounds" at party level 100, all bosses sit in a 450 to 520 base hp band (Hunger 800), no foe drops gold, no rest room in the four-level dungeon run. Details below.

**9740.** The best level. Ordered rooms (entrance, nave with twisted columns, portrait gallery, dining, salon, banquet, statues, trophy hall), no open rectangle of 5 by 5 anywhere (checked by script), ghosts in costume with period dress, ancestors that grin, sheeted windows, candelabra. Against it: the nave and entrance are a bare red carpet with gold lines (nave_end, entr_n); no ribbed vault from the columns is ever visible because the camera looks level; stained windows are only dark blue panes; the Lady's room (rest) is peach brick and green carpet, the only colour clash in the level; 65 foes (13 armour, 10 courtiers, 9 dancers, 7 carvers, 7 servants) on 1547 open squares is one foe per 24 squares and nothing in the nave is safe. The Grand Stair is two walk-through billboards and a flat lavender iron gate, not a stair.
**9750.** Weakest look. Walls are flat saturated cyan-blue brick with glowing mortar lines (cartoon, not black gothic), big flat ivy sprites fill the view like green blobs, bookcases stand in the Larder and Pantry (purple dripping books), a pink blob hangs in the Scullery, the Maids' Loft is bright ochre brick with rust streaks, 36 floating gargoyles, 21 identical kitchen rat heaps, 9 copies of the same butler ghost, the Steward boss is the same ghost butler sprite as those nine. Gameplay and rooms are fine; the paint is not.
**9760.** Chapel (chequer floor, pews, candle chandelier), bell room and clock room are good. Library floor strip is a flat untextured green slab; walls are the same cyan-blue generated brick or green mossy brick; the east stair door is a blurry pixel padlock flanked by two giant floating gargoyles; 14 identical Pale Monk foes plus 6 identical priest ghosts; cloister garth ivy walls are blurry green blobs.
**9780.** Genuinely oppressive and readable (chains, racks, iron maidens, skeletons, torture room), but monochrome green, the same leaf-blob wall everywhere, 10 identical 3 by 3 cells with a twig-tree sprite and a bucket well in each, 13 Pale Turnkeys that are a plain grey man in a shirt and 13 Corpse Rats that are Oldhaven graveyard rats (plump, upright).
**9790.** The cistern is the weakest space in the set: a flat water plane, cube pillars wearing an ivy skirt, a stone slab strip as a "bridge" with no parapet, very little to see, long dark views. A flat 40 by 14 hall of water is exactly what the owner said he does not want. Leeches and drowned prisoners are good.
**9800.** The best dungeon. Corridor ring round the pit, faces on the walls, the Hunger rising out of the pit, the chained dais with the violet-lit strongbox (two skeletons, candelabra, steps) is a real moment. Still green on green, a big moss rock sprite, and twig bundles on 28 squares.

## Balance and pacing

Foe counts and density (foes per open square): 9740 65 (1:24), 9750 44 (1:24), 9760 37 (1:27), 9780 38 (1:20), 9790 23 (1:34), 9800 15 (1:27). Levels 101, 101, 101, 102, 103, 104; party level 100.
Stats (base hp, before level scaling): every ordinary foe 40 to 118 hp, 5 to 17 damage, 20 to 36 xp, gold 0 to 0. At party level 100 almost every ordinary foe is shown as DEADLY at 5 to 8 rounds (Whirling Dancer, Pale Turnkey, Shackled Dead, Book Wraith, Pale Monk, Cell Ghost); rats and bakers are HARD. Bosses: Stair Warden 500 hp (45 rounds), Antlered Lord 450, Pale Steward 480 (45 rounds), Pale Sacristan 450, Questioner 520, Hunger 800 (75 rounds, 35,986 hp), plus the Drowned Gaoler (spawned on the bridge). Escalation from the first level to the last is 3 levels and 1.6 times boss hp: too flat. Suggest ordinary foes at 9780 to 9800 with 1.3 to 1.6 times hp and a gaunt silhouette, bosses stepped 500 / 520 / 600 / 700 / 1000.
Foes and gold: no foe drops gold. Gold is from chests only (Sacristy 6,000; Oubliette chest 9,000), loose floor loot is 3 to 4 greater-mana potions and 6 to 9 gems per level, nothing else. Gear (pg_*) is on bosses, riddle chests and quests (26 pieces, see tools/PALE_SPIRE_GEAR.md): the plan is sound; I did not find a gear placement error.
Rest rooms: 9740 Lady's Withdrawing Room (10,8 warp), 9750 Maids' Loft, 9760 Almonry; none in 9780, 9790, 9800 (three levels, 76 foes, the deepest boss): add one at the 9780 south stair and one at the 9790 landing. No foe is inside any rest room or within 8 of the waystone (ps_test reach confirms).
Pacing, the key route: 9740 and 9750 and 9760 start with ghosts and little threat; the first clue is the barred door bump (pspClue 1 in ps_halls.js line 683 and ps_wings.js line 134), more clues in wings (ledger, hook board, library book), gaols (Garrick, Peck, gaoler log, scratch on cell wall) and the Oubliette strongbox is opened only after the Hunger is dead. ps_test key passes. The chain is good; its weakness is that the first clue is not visible at the doors themselves (all three look like a decoration padlock). Putting a sign "KEY BELOW" or a chalk mark on the door image would help. The route down is straight (9740 to 9750 screens passage, undercroft door (20,36), stair (40,36), stair (8,36)); the way back up lands in 9750 at (20,33) and the nearest locked stair is West Turret (6,3), 40 squares away, which is fair.
Level flow: 9740 is a loop-and-branch chain with 5 doors, no dead-end problems; 9750 has two parallel lanes and a good loop through kitchen and servants' hall; 9760 has the cloister loop (cloister, ossuary, bell room, clock room, chancel, nave) which is the best loop in the set; 9780 is a long hall plus two corridors, with a lot of backtracking down 3-wide lanes; 9790 is a single flat slab hall; 9800 is a tight ring and a pit, short and sharp. Dead ends with rewards exist (9780 service crawl, 9800 bone chamber, 9740 niches) but the chests in cells (8 gy_chest in 9780) read as a loot dump.

## What is genuinely good (keep)

- Painted ghosts (p_ghost_* set, translucent), the haunting scenes in 9740 (dining ghosts, waltz couples, bride) and the quotes ("Pass the salt", "More wine"), mirror and portrait ghosts (a_9790_10).
- Black gothic furniture: tables, pews, sideboards, candelabra, pulpit, organ, throne, grandfather clocks (GOOD_banquet_9740, GOOD_nave_aisle_9740).
- Hall of Statues (GOOD_hall_of_statues_9740), portrait gallery with frames and grinning ancestors.
- Cobwebs, spiders, red lanterns, candle pools (good scale, correct warm accent).
- Bell room (great bell) and clock room, chapel (GOOD_chapel_9760, GOOD_clock_room_9760), library shelves.
- Oubliette: the Hunger rising from the pit, chained key dais with the violet strongbox (GOOD_key_dais_9800, GOOD_hunger_pit_9800), bone piles, shackled skeletons.
- Kitchen (GOOD_kitchen_9750): hanging pans, ghost staff, hearth, the sword racks and armour stands.
- Secret rooms (Painters' Closet, niches, service crawl, bone chamber) and a key chain that is wired and tested.
- No open 5 by 5 floor anywhere in 9740, 9750 (one 5 by 5 corner), 9760; room sizes are right.

Repaint walls of 9750, 9760, 9780 to 9800 in the 9740 black stone with painted moss/ivy variants. 4. Dresser: refuse dust sheets that seal a square. 5.
