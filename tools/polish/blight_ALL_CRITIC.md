# Blighted Lands: full critic pass (29 Oct 2026)

Method: own server on 8610, one headless Chrome at a time (swiftshader, 900x600, the game's normal size), jump links ?town=blightN, 3 to 4 stills per outdoor place and 2 to 3 per interior set, about 75 stills in all. Every still was looked at (as 2x2 contact sheets, scale .62). No game files were edited. The scale is AAA dungeon crawler (Grimrock 2, Dark Souls area design, Diablo IV, BG3 exploration). Rain and weather are random per arrival, so some stills are dry and some wet.

General verdict: the zone has a real identity (rotted green and red storm skies, painted props, a lot of writing to read) but it is held back by murk and sameness. Most outdoor stills are a brown road in the lower third, a flat grey band of haze in the middle and a few dark silhouettes. Interiors are clearly stronger than exteriors. Nothing crashes: no console errors in any run.

## Place by place

### 1 Toll House (9610) and the toll house room (9611): 5.5
Works: the cobbled toll yard with iron gate and crow tree is a good first read; the shrine candle tree with Sister Anwen is the best single composition of the place (gold ! marker, pale nun against a dark willow); the toll house room is warm, readable, with a skeleton warden in front of the fire.
Problems, ranked:
1. The east entrance is flanked by two identical pink bulb growths (`bl_grow_1`) dead centre on both sides of the road. They are the only saturated colour in the frame, read as organs, and the symmetry makes them look like a placed gate, not nature.
2. The graves view is a single huge flat slab (WARDEN) filling the middle with a wall of identical headstones behind it: no depth layers, no focal prop. Grass slope on the left is empty.
3. Mid distance is a grey band: the road, haze and dead hedges all share one value, so the toll house itself does not read from the gate.
4. Foreground black cut-out willows at the shrine are pure black blobs with no rim light; they eat the lower corners.
5. Sky is flat overcast with no cloud shape in the dry roll; the storm sky only helps when it rains.

### 2 Ashenfold Farm (9620) with kitchen (9621), cellar (9622): 5
Works: the kitchen is the best interior of the first two places (dead family at supper, two ghost figures, ! marker readable); the skull-pod tree at the east gate is a strong silhouette; the fungus monolith in the dead field is odd and memorable.
Problems, ranked:
1. The farmhouse front is a flat wall of identical dark windows with no light in any of them and no door shape visible in the rain. It reads as a wall texture, not a house. Lit windows would fix half of this zone.
2. The yard and fields are large empty green planes; heavy rain wash plus dust fog flattens them to one value.
3. Pink bulb growths again at the gate (same as the toll house): repeated hero prop, now noticeable across places.
4. The fungus monolith stands alone in the field with nothing to anchor it, nothing near its base (no shadow, no litter) so it looks pasted in.
5. Cellar floor is a big empty stone plane with a single bone dot; the props all sit on the back wall.

### 3 Church of the Hollow Saint (9630) with nave, crypt, belfry: 6.5
Works: the painted church at the end of the flagged path with two tall lamps is a proper Dark Souls style landmark; red storm sky works here; the skull tree and the screaming tree beside the plague pit are the best prop pairing in the zone; nave ghosts with a chandelier glow and the altar scene with the priest are very good.
Problems, ranked:
1. Church front is one flat picture on a flat cobbled path with no steps, no porch depth, no side buttress; the "upside down bells" did not read in any of my four stills (no bell shape visible from the approach).
2. The plague pit view is cut off at the left by the giant skull tree trunk: the frame has no clear subject except a pile of bones at the lower middle.
3. Nave floor checker pattern is high contrast and busy, competing with the ghosts; the far wall is empty.
4. Graveyard stones are all the same grey slab with no variation in height or lean; fence line behind is a wall of repeats.
5. Crypt is a dim green plane with two trestles; pale and low detail on the floor.

### 4 Gallowmere (9640) with Osk's house (9641), Reeve's hall (9642): 6
Works: purple green sky and fog sell the fen; the causeway with lamp and wisp is atmospheric; flooded Reeve's hall with wave lines on the floor and the banner is a standout interior; Osk's house is warm and has a readable ! and readable slate.
Problems, ranked:
1. The Fen Wisp at 60,38 filled a third of the frame as a huge green smear while standing on the causeway: scale far beyond the other props; reads as a rendering bug, not a creature.
2. Water is a flat dark teal plane with no shine, no reflection of the lamps or sky; the fen is mostly empty flat colour at the horizon.
3. Houses are normal Wenlock Tudor houses on the ground (the stilt house stand-in did not appear in my stills): nothing says "fen village".
4. Dead tree and reed clumps repeat at the same scale and spacing along the shore (g4c): noise, not composition.
5. Village view (g4d) has a pretty ghost woman but the plaza stones are a flat grey; the gibbet tree was not in any of my four angles.

### 5 Bone Orchard (9650) with pressing shed, harvest hall: 5.5
Works: the white claw trees framing the thatched barn at the east gate are striking; orchard rows give a real sense of rank and depth; the harvest hall table with big book is a good composed room.
Problems, ranked:
1. The skull-apple trees are not visible as such: the orchard is white dead trees plus a tinted fog, so the zone's name prop is missing from the stills. Only the growth skull tree appears as an accident.
2. The Reaper-Farmer boss at the bell: his legs fill the frame at 2 squares, the bell is cut off, and the lamp and cobbles behind are a mess. Nothing frames the boss from the yard approach.
3. The "crypt" is a small Wenlock house with a columned porch beside a pyramid roof: it reads as a pub, not a crypt of a lost patrol.
4. Orchard trees are drawn at one size and same dark; there is no fruit colour, no light.
5. Pressing shed floor: huge empty planks, only a bone pile as foreground.

### 6 Lanternrest Waystation (9660) with inn, hall: 4.5
Works: the cosy inn with chandelier, bar and bottle shelves; the captain's hall with banners and table are clean readable rooms; the face tree and skull tree pair at the Road-Maw is memorable.
Problems, ranked:
1. Outdoors the fog and rain wash are so thick that the fort wall is not visible at all in two of four stills: a tiny house at the far end of a grey plain. The fort is the zone's "safe place" and reads as nothing.
2. Rain overlay greys the whole picture; the lamps and warm windows promised by the notes do not show against it.
3. Inn and hall rooms are big and nearly empty: one row of counters with a lot of bare floor, no figures visible in the stills.
4. Face tree at the Road-Maw is too large for its place, towering over the camp, and sits on a bare patch with no lead-in.
5. Roads are brown flat bands; the cobbled yard is flat grey with no dressing.

### 7a Barrow Hill (9670): 6
Works: pale gravel road, lamps and the pale front of the Vault with statues and green light are the clearest AAA-style landmark composition outside the church; the skull signpost is a good readable object; tents give a camp.
Problems, ranked:
1. Twisted trees and growths are placed in clumps that read as noise rather than a deliberate wood: the first still (road and tree clump) has four silhouettes of the same colour overlapping.
2. `bl_twist_1` at (52,52) is walled in (see Known issues): the pale vine wall and trunk fill the frame at that spot.
3. Gravel path is a uniform pale band; the long slope up to the vault front is not conveyed (no steps or walls).
4. Red sky is saturated against green ground; the only mid value is the fog.
5. Large parts of the lower hill are empty ground between the clumps.

### 7b Vault of Maldrake floors (9671 to 9678): 7
Works: the best zone for lighting. Torch brackets on warm brick, blue floor of the Bell Gallery with real bells, red Hall of Underhill with ghost shapes in the dark, the "Ringers' Room" hanging signs all read. Props lie on the floors (boots, books) and give story.
Problems, ranked:
1. Torch lit walls clip to pure yellow white in the Ossuary and stair (9671, 9672): flat blown slabs with no brick detail.
2. Maldrake's Sanctum (9677) is a huge empty dark hall: candelabras in a line, a tiny figure far off on a tile, no scale, no floor interest. As the climax it is the weakest room.
3. Corridor walls are long unbroken single colour planes (red, blue, yellow): needs wall props, niches, door recesses.
4. Colour coding is strong but too saturated per floor, so the vault looks like several themed mazes, not one building.
5. Enemies at 3 to 5 squares are tiny and unlit (ghost group in the Hall of Underhill is almost silhouette).

### 8 Hollowbell Abbey (9680) with chapter house, refectory, belfry: 6.5
Works: the wrought iron abbey gate and the painted church with its Gothic door are the best architecture pictures in the zone; the well in the cloister with a ghost-lit church behind is a good set piece; the refectory with the oversized iron chandelier has presence.
Problems, ranked:
1. The abbey church in the cloister is the same flat painted picture as the Church of the Hollow Saint, greyed. Repeat of the zone's one painted building (also the chapter house and tower).
2. Road to the abbey is a narrow brown strip between tree silhouettes: the abbey is not visible from the road.
3. Chapter house floor is the same bright checker as the nave: vertigo and no focal object but a lectern.
4. The belfry is a small dark room with the Abbot as a small grey figure at the far wall.
5. The chandelier in the refectory overlaps the table and monk (top crop): reads as a scale error.

### 9 Rookery Mill (9690) with grinding floor and cap: 6
Works: the Parliament Stones with seven rooks and a ! marker is a legitimately fresh set piece; the windmill among twisted trees on the hill is the zone's best silhouette; the interior crow nest (cap) has a lot of life.
Problems, ranked:
1. The brown red haze tint on the sky makes the whole picture mud coloured; the mill is nearly the same value as the sky.
2. Grinding floor interior: a single runestone, a cauldron and one ghost in a big wooden barn: props too few, too far apart.
3. In the cap the rook swarm covers a third of the frame in front of the camera: no way to see the room.
4. Black iron fence at the bridge is a repeated stripe with no gaps or variations and dominates the view.
5. Road to the mill is a flat brown band with no wheel ruts or puddles.

### 10 Pale Bridge and Gate of Sale (9700) with gatehouse and wall walk: 5.5
Works: first view of the gate with SALE THE GATE sign and the grey gate with lamp is strong; the iron gate seen from the bridge with the Pale Knight in a rain streak is dramatic.
Problems, ranked:
1. The Pale Knight in the middle of the bridge is a giant flat armoured cut-out that fills the screen at 3 squares and blocks the gate: scale far too large for a person.
2. The bridge ravine is invisible: the bridge reads as a flat paved road, with no drop, no parapet depth and no mist.
3. The SALE THE GATE board is cut off by the screen edge in the arrival still.
4. The west end of the zone is a dark wall with an arch: no foreground, no prop; the rain makes the picture a grey gradient.
5. The gatehouse and wall walk rooms are plain: one guard, one rack, one fire, large empty planks.

### Sale (600): 4
Works: the 1-wide streets with shop signs (Beadletter's Emporium legible) are a good "plague quarter" start; the ghost on the bone pile at the square is the best single picture; the crow on the gibbet cage and the bone heaps are well placed.
Problems, ranked:
1. The town is almost unreadable: grave fog and rain fade everything past 3 squares to the same dark grey. Three of eight stills were nothing but wall.
2. The gibbet is a hanging cage lantern (a birdcage with a skeleton): it reads as a cage, not a gibbet.
3. Walls and doors are all the same grey stone, gothic arches repeated: no variation in facade, no signage at distance.
4. The ghost villagers are semi-transparent blobs at the same height and colour; they do not read as people.
5. Roads are 1 wide in places so every view is a corridor; there are no wide sight lines to any landmark.

### Interiors as a set: 6.5
Strong: nave with choir ghosts, flooded hall, kitchen, belfry, Bell Gallery. Weak: Lanternrest rooms and gatehouse (empty), the sanctum, the cellar.

## Known issues

1. **bl_twist_3 stray lightning bolt: CONFIRMED in the picture.** `assets/ds_bl_twist_3.png` (223 x 263) has about 138 near-white opaque pixels forming a thin forked bolt between the two split halves, rows 0 to about 100, columns about 105 to 135. On a dark background it is plainly visible. In the game it shows between the two trunk halves of the tree on the hill (57,22), (2,21), (6,39) and in Sale (34,53), (8,19), (33,68). It is subtle at distance, plain at 2 squares. Fix: clear alpha for that bolt (a flood fill from the top of the picture over the pixels with min channel above 225, or erase rows 0 to 105 for columns 100 to 135), or re-cut from the Gemini sheet with `tools/polish/blg_cut.py`.
2. **bl_twist_1 at (52,52) on the Vault hill: CONFIRMED walled in.** Its four neighbours in 4-connectivity are all blocking: (52,51) `bl_twist_5`, (51,52) `ltree2`, (53,52) `ww_stump`, (52,53) `bl_twist_3`. (53,51) `gy_slab`, (51,53) `bl_grow_1` and (53,53) `gy_dead` also block, only (51,51) is free. The party cannot stand beside it, so the talking examine text is unreachable. Fix: remove `ltree2` at (51,52) (or `ww_stump` at 53,52), or give `blight_growths.js` a step that checks talking props have a free orthogonal neighbour with a free 2x2.
3. **`links` test fails for the Vault: root cause is the test, not the game.** Reproduced with `BL_PORT=8610 python tools/blight56_test.py links`: place 7 reports `E: None, W: None, ok: False`. `tools/blight56_test.py` line 65 and `tools/blight_test.py` line 60 find doors with `KH_DOOR[...].bl`. The Vault's two road gates are made in `src/desert/vault_hill.js` line 80 as `KH_DOOR[gid]={vt:'road',dir}` (side and lvl are not set, there is no `bl` flag) and handled by `vtRoad` in `src/desert/vault_core.js` line 169 to 172, which asks `vtNeighbour` / `VT.neighbour`. `src/desert/blight_glue.js` sets `VT.neighbour` and `p.built`, `p.fromE`, `p.fromW` correctly. The actual game links are right: `vtNeighbour('east')` returns 9660 and `vtNeighbour('west')` 9680, `blWestDest(place 6)` is 9670, `blEastDest(place 8)` is 9670, `?town=blight7` lands in 9670 at the east gate. Fix: in both tests treat place 7 specially (read doors with `d.vt==='road'` and compare `d.dir` east or west to `vtNeighbour(dir).id`), or add `bl:true, side:'E'|'W', lvl, at` fields to the two KH_DOOR objects in vault_hill.js line 80 (harmless, `vtRoad` ignores them). Side note: `BL_ROAD` row 7 says W 72, H 64 but the hill is 64 x 56 (VTH); only the table is wrong.
4. Upside-down bells, stilt houses, hanged figures: did not appear in any of my stills (no bell shape on the church approach, houses on the ground at Gallowmere, no gibbet tree in my four angles). They remain stand-ins per the notes; not judged by eye.

## Stand-in pictures ranked for new Gemini art (what hurts most, by what is seen)

1. Lit windows: affects Ashenfold, Lanternrest, Sale, Hollowbell and the Vault front; at night and in rain every facade is a wall of dead windows. Biggest, most widespread gain.
2. Sale gibbet: a cage lantern with a skeleton is the town's central set piece and does not read as a gibbet.
3. Painted Maldrake: the climax of the Vault is a tiny toned wizard on an empty tile.
4. Ghost villagers: Sale's population is translucent blobs; the quarter has no people.
5. Skull-apple tree: Bone Orchard's title prop is absent from the view.
6. Stilt houses: Gallowmere is a fen with ordinary houses.
7. Upside-down bell: not seen on the approach; low visual cost.
8. Hanged figures: not seen; scarecrow totems already stand in acceptably.

## Ten highest impact fixes overall

1. Thin the fog and the rain wash outdoors (Lanternrest, Sale, Pale Bridge, Ashenfold): keep silhouettes readable at 6 to 10 squares; rain should add streaks, not grey everything. The single biggest uplift.
2. Painted lit windows for all houses (see list above).
3. Remove the bl_twist_3 bolt and open the (52,52) pocket (two small asset and data edits).
4. Fix the Vault links test (tests lines 60 and 65) so the suite is green again.
5. Cut the paired pink bulb growths from the toll and farm gates, or tone them to the palette.
6. Scale pass: Pale Knight (bridge), Fen Wisp, Reaper at the bell, face tree at Lanternrest, rook swarm in the cap are all far bigger than their frame allows.
7. Give the Vault sanctum a real focal set: dais, painted Maldrake, light shaft, fewer repeated candelabras.
8. Build the Lanternrest wall as a visible thing (stone texture, battlement edge, lamps along the top) and put figures in the inn and hall.
9. Prop variety and clustering in woods and graveyards: break repeating tree and gravestone silhouettes; add value contrast (rim light, pale ground) between layers.
10. Fix overexposed torch walls in the Vault upper floors (clamp light on brick).
