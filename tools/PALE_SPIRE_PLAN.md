# The Pale Spire: master plan and contract (29 Oct 2026)

The Pale Spire is the Pale Sovereign's castle in the Pale Lands (rating 100 and up), the last part of the west. It is a BIG multi-level gothic castle: many floors, each with wings and towers, with outdoor areas, mazes and forest atriums, and the Sovereign at the top. Only the FIRST BUILD is built now (gate, courtyard, ground floor, dungeons). Everything above is outline with reserved ids.

Read first: `CLAUDE.md` (art is painted, front on, never SVG for creatures; no em dashes), `NEXT_PART_PLAN.md`, `tools/BLIGHT_NOTES.md` (the kit `blKit`, `blRegister`, helpers), `tools/SALE_NOTES.md`, `tools/VAULT_NOTES.md` (the interior floor maker `vtMake` in `src/desert/vault_build.js`, locks, plates, bosses, quests, rings), `tools/MAP_BRIEF_SPIRE.md` (that is the brief for the ORANGE Spire parchment maps; it is the model for the Pale Spire's maps).

The shared numbers are in code: `src/desert/ps_ids.js` (table `PSP`: levels, sizes, ratings, every link with exact squares). It is the contract. If you must change a number, tell package 6 and change it in that file only. The global name `PS` is already taken (powers_scaling.js), so all globals of this part start with `psp` / `PSP`; ids as strings (creatures, items, people, decor keys, quests) start with `ps`.

## A. The whole castle, by floor (outline)

Real castles have these parts (checked against castle glossaries): gatehouse with portcullis and murder holes, barbican in front of it, curtain wall with a wall walk, towers, bailey (courtyard), keep or donjon, great hall with a screens passage to the buttery, pantry and kitchens, solar (private family room), chapel, great chamber and state bedchambers, garderobes (latrines in the wall), undercroft (storage under the hall), wine cellar, cistern, oubliette (a pit cell entered from above), torture chamber, armoury, mews (hawks), stables, scriptorium and library, cloister, a gallery of portraits, an orangery or winter garden. The Pale Spire uses all of them.

Ids 9710 to 9998 are reserved for the Pale Spire (checked free; 9999 stays unused). A block of ten is one place: the first id is its main map, the others are its rooms, towers or floors. Rating = level of the creatures (the engine spreads them). Ratings run 100 to 115.

Wings and towers by corner (so later floors line up): the castle is a U round the courtyard, facing south. North range = the main block (Great Hall in the middle). West wing (service: kitchens, armoury) and east wing (quiet: cloister, chapel, library) run south along each side of the court. Four corner towers stand at the ends of the curtain wall: NW Raven Tower, NE Bell Tower, SW Alchemist's Tower, SE Moon Tower (the gatehouse towers flank the gate between the two south towers). The two turret stairs (NW corner of the west wing, NE corner of the east wing) are the service stairs up; the Grand Stair is the state stair. All three need the Second Floor Key.

The Sovereign's fate (killed, redeemed, both) and the use of the Violet Tower are open design questions (NEXT_PART_PLAN.md); the plan keeps the summit generic.

### B3. The key item and where it is hidden
Item `pskey2`, "The Second Floor Key" (quest kind, key icon, pale silver with a violet jewel in the bow; description plain: "A tall silver key with a violet stone in the bow. It opens the three stair doors to the second floor of the Pale Spire.").

Hiding place (package 4, level 9760): the SACRISTY, a small room (58,22)-(68,32) north of the chapel nave, behind a hidden door in the chapel's north wall at (62,33) that looks like plain wall. It opens when the party pulls the candle bracket at (45,35) in the chapel (a sconce that is slightly different, with a light gold ring). In the sacristy stands the Sacristan's reliquary chest holding the key; guarding it is the mini boss The Pale Sacristan (a ghost, rating 102). Taking the key sets `G.flags.psp.key2`. Clues, all optional, any one is enough to find it: (1) the Steward's ledger in the Steward's Office (9750): "Upper keys returned to the Sacristan at dusk, as always"; (2) a book in the Library (9760), "The Sacristan's Habit": "he kept the keys where the candles are"; (3) Ansel Peck or Hob Garrick in the dungeons (quest psq30 reward) says the Sacristan hid something in the chapel before the guards took him; (4) a faint gold ring on the one odd bracket (visible when close). The jump link `?town=palespirekey` lands in the sacristy.

All three stair doors check the bag for `pskey2`. Without it the door says: "An iron-bound door with a silver lock. It will not move. The key must be somewhere on this floor." (the first time the party bumps one of them, quest psq10 "Up the Grand Stair" is offered by a note; see package 3).

### B4. The look
* Pale stone, silver and violet light, a cold gothic palette. Walls: pale ashlar (#cfd0de lit, #6a6884 in shadow), silver-grey trim (#dfe6f2), violet shadow (#46406a), dark slate roofs (#3c3a52), gold only in small touches (frames, candle rings, key bows #d8c070). Lamps and torches burn silver-white (#dfe8ff) and violet (#b48cff); put them in `lv._ps.lcol`.
* WARM CANDLELIGHT (#f0b070) is used ONLY in the quiet rest rooms and the prisoners' cells that are friendly. Rule for players: warm light means safe. Everywhere else the light is cold. (The kitchen fires burn cold white.)
* Outdoors: a pale lilac-white sky with no sun disc, a permanent still twilight (`day` fixed near .5, no night swing), thin silver mist low on the ground, slow falling motes like ash (reuse `weatherNow` from blight_kit.js and give it pale colours), lawns of pale grey-green grass (#a8b8a4), white gravel, silver-leaved hedges (tint existing hedges), no black crows (the Rookery was the crows' place; ravens only in the mews).
* Floors: pale flagstones, black and white marble chequer in the halls, a violet carpet runner down the centre of halls and galleries, wet dark flags and straw in dungeons.
* Ambience and sound (package 6, `ps_sound.js`, made with `tone` and `nz` like the vault and Sale; reuse samples `church_bell1/2` at rate .4 for a far slow bell): outdoors a low wind through arches and a faint glass chime every 20 to 40 s; halls a slow pad of open fifths and a far music box playing a wrong waltz (the dancing dead), footsteps echo; kitchens a cold crackle and the slow drip of a copper pan; chapel a held organ note and a candle hush; rest rooms a quiet warm hum and no wind (a calm line); dungeons drips, a chain clink, a far moan, a rat patter; cistern running water.
* Named plaques and boards in plain capitals as in the vault: THE GREAT GATE, THE COURT OF STATUES, THE GREAT HALL, THE GALLERY OF PORTRAITS, THE KITCHENS, THE ARMOURY, THE CHAPEL, THE LIBRARY, THE UNDERCROFT GAOLS and so on (see each package).
* Every level gets a parchment area map in the style of `maps_os*.js` and the vault maps (package 6, `ps_maps.js`, with `reveal:true`, rooms named from `mapNames`, no spoilers: no hidden doors, no bosses).
