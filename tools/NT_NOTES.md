# The Never-Ending Tower: notes for the next session

Branch `nt-tower` (cut from `main`). An endless tower north of the Pale Spire: floor n (1, 2, 3, ...) is built from its number alone, rated 99 + n (floor 1 is level 100), made of a mixture of dense mazes, mazes with rooms, sparse halls and a few special shapes, each floor in its own colour and look.

## Files (src/desert, in ORDER just before `blight_titans.js`; run `python tools/inject.py` after editing)
- `nt_look.js`  constants (`NT`), `ntIs`, seeds (`ntSeed`, `ntRng`), the palette of a floor (`ntPalette`: golden-angle hue, so neighbours are far apart), the look set (`ntLook`: layout kind, wall stone and pattern, floor kinds, ceiling, height, ambient light, name), the wall tiles (`ntWallTiles`: painted graveyard stone tinted, with one of 12 code patterns drawn on it), stairs and niche tiles, floors and ceilings, and `ntRelease` (gives a floor's textures back).
- `nt_gen.js`  the plans (`ntGen(n, look)`): pure data. dense, semi, sparse, spiral, cross, ring, caves; the shop strip; stairs and stone (`ntgStairs`). Test without a browser: `node tools/nt_node.js check` or `node tools/nt_node.js ascii 37`.
- `nt_foes.js`  creature families (`NT_FAMS`), `ntfFamilies()`, `ntfPool`, guardians (`ntfGuardian`, `ntg_<type>`), chest loot (`lootFor` wrapper), the shop cycle (`NT_SHOPS`, `ntKeeper`).
- `nt_core.js`  `ntBuildFloor(n)` (a level object like the vault's), art loading (`ntLoad`), arrival hooks (wraps of `goToLevel`, `jumpToLevel`, `restore`, `restoreNow`, `teleportTo`, `khBump`, `weatherNow`, `setupLights`, `wdDoorLevel`), floors given back (`ntEvict`), sound.
- `nt_map.js`  the entrance hall, the great door, the world map entry, the teleport network menu, achievements, stats line, Go to area rows, jump links, the parchment map.
- Core edits in `index.html` (both tiny, in `jumpFromUrl`): the party level `ntJumpLevel(town,q)` and the call `ntAreas(AREAS)`.

## Ids and numbers
- `NT.BASE = 1e9`. Level id of floor n = `NT.BASE + n` (`ntIdOf(n)`, `ntFloorOf(id)`, `ntIs(id)`). Floor 0 is the entrance hall `The Crooked Tower` (id `NT.BASE`). Rating of floor n: `99 + n` (`LEVEL_RATING` is set when a floor is built). 1e9 was chosen because ids from 40000 up are the game's locked houses (`camp.js`: `40000 + lv*10000 + y*100 + x`, never above about 1e8) and nothing else goes near 1e9.
- Floors are NOT in `LEVEL_NAMES` (so the dungeon list of the achievements page is not flooded); `L.name`, `L.label` and `L.teleName` hold the name, `LEVEL_RATING` the level.
- Flags: `G.flags.nt = {deepest, best:{floor,level}, ms:{10:1,...}, open, debug}`. Nothing else is saved by the tower itself.

## How a floor is seeded and saved
Everything about a floor comes from `ntSeed(n, salt)` (a 32 bit hash of the floor number, usable far above 2^32): the kind (`ntKindOf`: even floors draw from dense/spiral/ring/caves, odd floors from semi/sparse/cross, so two floors in a row never share a kind; floor 1 is semi), the palette, the plan, the pool of creatures, every prop. The order of `enemies`, `items` and `decor` is the same each time, so the game's own level state (`G.lst`: items taken, chests opened with `contents`, the dead by index, torches taken) works unchanged and a save inside floor 12345 loads: `restore` builds the floor first. The dead stay dead (nothing returns to the tower). Floors more than 3 away from the party are deleted from `LEVELS` and their textures are reused (`ntEvict`, `ntRelease`; the `addTex` wrapper takes numbers off `NT.freeTex`): 240 floors in a row keep the texture count flat (test `mem`).
A save grows about 300 bytes per floor visited (G.lst); 240 floors is 74 KB.

## What a floor holds
- Torches: every wall square beside open floor is a candidate (rooms first, then bends, then corridors); one torch for each 2.3 squares (3.0 in a one-wide corridor), measured between the squares they light, so the two sides of a thin wall each get theirs. About 150 to 470 per floor. Flame sprite and light colour follow the floor's colour (`ntPalette`: flame 0 orange, 1 red, 2 purple, 3 blue, 4 yellow).
- Braziers (solid, with a light) beside walls in rooms and bends; candle niches in the walls.
- Chests (`gy_chest`, `chest`, four vases), about one per 42 squares, in dead ends and rooms; gold = `L * 40 * (0.6 to 2)` (x6 for the guardian's chest, `goldAmt` marks it), potions, supermana, armour, rings, a gem. Loose gems and potions on the floor; bones and urns as dressing.
- Creatures: two to four families per floor (graveyard, blight, Sale, witch wood, hollow castle, deserts, wild, hives, marsh, amethyst well, Pale Spire; see `NT_FAMS`). `ntfOk` removes bosses, creatures that stand above the party (`aboveParty > 0`), wanderers, patrols, minis, quest creatures, mimics and creatures without a picture. Every creature stands at `99 + n`. None within 7 squares of the arrival or the stone. Three to five guard the stairs up. Every tenth floor: a guardian (`ntg_<type>`, five times the health, more damage, strongest of the pool) and a great chest.
- A teleport stone three to five steps from the arrival (`L.tele`), so `placeTeleporter` and the game's network use it. Stairs down: a door tile beside the arrival square; stairs up: far away (at least 82% of the longest walk).
- Every seventh floor: a safe shop room in a strip at the foot of the plan (`plan.shop`), a keeper (`khNpc` `nt_k_<key>`), barrels and sacks. `NT_SHOPS` is the cycle (40 shops: general, armoury, weaponsmith, apothecary, bookseller, jeweller, tailor, butcher, baker, alchemist, inn with beds, then every other `KH_STOCK` kind; `NT_SHOP_SKIP` lists the stocks left out as duplicates). Floor 7 is shop 0.

## The way in, and how to hook the castle finale
- **The tower is always open (no lock, no quest).** `ntUnlocked()` is always true. The only gate is the party's level: `ntLevelOk()` (nt_map.js) needs `partyLevel()` (the highest hero) of at least `NTM.MINLVL` = 100, or the debug flag (`G.flags.nt.debug`, set by the jump links, so `?town=neverending` at party level 99 still works). The great door and the Crooked Road's tower gate (ps_road.js wraps `khBump`) refuse a lower party with `ntTooLow()`: "The way in is barred. Only a party of level 100 or more may enter the Crooked Tower." Going in by Go to area or the stone is not gated (those jumps raise the party to the level of the target).
- **The way in: the Crooked Road (level 9715, `src/desert/ps_road.js`, 48 x 64, rating 100, inside the Pale range).** Court of Statues (9730) east postern (60,30) -> road start (40,59) -> five-wide road in switchbacks past the Bone Field, the Wayside Shrine (free rest, safe), the Glade of Crosses and the Leaning Lamps -> forecourt and the tower gate (30,4) -> the entrance hall `NT.BASE` at `NT.ENT.arrive` (18,26,0). The hall's south gate (`nt:'exit'`) leads back to (30,6) facing south. The links are in `PSP.LINKS` (ps_ids.js; the tower link has `ext:1`, there is no twin in the table because the hall's gate is code). Rules kept in the builder: the middle three squares of the road are empty, solid props stand 3 or more squares off the centre line, creatures (8, all in glades) 3 or more squares off, no houses, no animals. `?town=crookedroad` (`PSP.JUMPS`) jumps to the start; the Go to area list shows it with the other Pale Lands rows. ps_road.js is after ps_glue.js in `tools/inject.py` ORDER so its `khBump` wrap runs before the glue's door rule.
- The entrance hall (`ntBuildEntrance`) has the great door (north wall) and a south gate to the Crooked Road (9715). (No seal any more, see above.)
- The Pale Spire's last boss needs no hook any more: nothing is locked. `ntUnlock()` was removed (`G.flags.nt.open` in old saves is ignored).
- World map: entry `neverending` ("The Never-Ending Tower") at (-906, 98) north of the Pale Spire, a road from the Spire. Icon: three stacked, slightly shifted copies of the Spire picture (`i_spire`) as a stand-in. A painted icon is wanted: name it **`i_neverending`** (add it to the atlas and set `WMN_SPEC.neverending`). Floors belong to the tower on the map (`teleLocOf` wrapper); the padlock shows while `lv` is empty.
- The painted front of the tower is wanted: **`nt_tower_front`**. Put it in `assets/nt/` with `assets/nt/manifest.json` `{ "tower_front": {"file": "tower_front.webp"} }`; `ntLoadOwn` loads `IMG.nt_tower_front` and the great door uses it (four tiles wide) in place of the graveyard's gothic front.
- Teleport menu: standing on a woken stone and pressing T, the tower on the world map opens a menu (`ntTeleNode`): go to the highest woken floor, next stone up or down, about 10 up or down, "Longer leaps" (100 and 1000, only once a floor 100 stone is woken). Only floors whose stone is woken are listed. `teleLevelsAt(tower)` returns `ntHandful()`: the entrance, floor 1, the highest, floors within 10 of the party, multiples of 25 and (up to the highest) of 10.
- Achievements: the page gets a section "The Never-Ending Tower" (deepest floor as "Floor N, level L" with the party's level at that time; reached floor 10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000); the stats page has a "Never-Ending Tower" line; a message and a fanfare at each new deepest floor and each milestone.
- Jump links: `?town=neverending` (the entrance hall, party level 99) and `?town=neverending&floor=N` (floor N, party level 99 + N, capped at 300 so that the jump does not level up for ever; `&lvl=` overrides). They set `G.flags.nt.debug`, which passes the level gate. The Go to area list has a section for the tower (the entrance row, Floor 1 and the deepest floor, always listed).

## Looks
Twelve wall patterns (ashlar, brick, panels, flutes, studs, lattice, runes, herringbone, planks, scales, chevrons, rough) drawn on the four painted stones (grey, bone, black, red only where the hue is red), tinted by the floor's hue; six floor kinds (flag, slate, cobble, gravel, bone, dirt) plus code-made checker, diamond and stripe floors for rooms; five tinted ceilings; wall height 1.35 to 1.85; ambient 0.07 to 0.13. Two floors in a row never share a kind, and `ntLooksDiffer` (hue at least 50 degrees apart and a different pattern, stone or kind) is tested for floors 1 to 60. Sound: the vault's drips, creaks and moans, plus two voices per kind (`ntsTick`).

## Tests (own server, `python -m http.server 8624` from the worktree root; env `BTA_CHROME`, `NT_PORT`)
`python tools/nt_test.py <mode>`, one mode at a time: `build` (floors 1 to 60, 100, 1000, 100000), `foes`, `shops`, `save`, `tele`, `dash`, `walk` (real collision on 14 floors of all 7 kinds, up and down the stairs), `mem`, `seal` (level gate: great door and road gate refuse below 100, accept at 100, the debug flag passes; the south gate leads to the road), `shots` (screenshots; `python tools/nt_sheet.py folder out.png 3` makes a contact sheet, `tools/nt_crop.py` enlarges a part). `tools/nt_probe.py` runs a bit of JS in the game, `tools/nt_node.js` checks plans without a browser.

## Known gaps and ideas
- No painted art: the tower icon and the tower front are stand-ins (above). The floors use the graveyard's painted stones, stairs and props; the shops' keepers are Oldhaven's folk.
- Very large levels: `enemyStat` is finite at levels 100, 1000 and 1e6, but the game's balance code gives creatures little health and huge damage up there (floor 100000: 3,000 health, 400,000 damage). Not touched.
- Nothing returns after the dead are dead; a floor is cleared for good. The party's level is not raised by the tower itself.
- The parchment map only draws what the party has seen.
- The gates and doors of rooms are plain openings (the plans have no real doors).

- Floor finish (this session): the route from the arrival to the stairs up (`V.route`, a shortest walk) is kept clear of braziers, chests, urns and creatures (`build` tests it for floors 1 to 60 and three huge ones); the floor tint now leans 55 degrees away from the wall hue and is less saturated, so floors read as stone under coloured walls. Wall torches are wall squares and never stand on the walk.
- The tower's great door (4 slices of `assets/nt/tower_front.webp`, 1572 x 900, matches 4 tiles x 2.3 high) was checked in screenshots (`python tools/nt_test.py` shots, or load `?town=neverending` and stand at (18,6)); emit raised from .32 to .6 so the front reads in the hall's dim light.
