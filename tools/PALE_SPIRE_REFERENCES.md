# Pale Spire: owner's reference pictures (29 Oct 2026)

Seven pictures in tools/ref_spire/ (ref8.jpg to ref14.jpg). LOOK at them. The owner wants some of this built into the design. What each shows and what to take from it:

* ref8 (grand entrance): a wide flight of steps climbing between ivy-hung walls to an arched double door under an ornate carved window and balcony; towers with pointed caps behind; stone urns on the balustrade. Take: the castle front as a stepped, tiered approach (steps up to the main door, balustrades with urns, a carved rose window above, ivy).
* ref9 (castle on a rock, from above): clusters of conical slate-roofed towers of different heights and sizes, steep gabled roofs, a tiered terrace, and below it a bastion with three arched tunnel gateways and stairs climbing on both sides to the terrace. Take: the silhouette (many conical towers, gables, banners), the lower gate bastion with several arches and side stairs, terraces on different levels.
* ref10 (terraced courtyard): a sunken round pond with a fountain at the middle of a flagged courtyard, arcades of arches with columns, balconies with stone balustrades, long ivy, potted ferns in urns, hedges, a curved stair up the right side, tall towers with conical caps behind. Take: the pond/fountain, arcaded galleries, balconies, potted ferns, ivy curtains, clipped hedges, curved stairs.
* ref11 (night, red and violet): a red wrought iron gate at the end of a cobbled bridge path between low stone walls, roses climbing the walls, lit red windows in green-grey towers with red conical roofs, black-blue sky with red storm clouds, small lamps. Take: the mood of the GATE approach (red and violet storm sky, iron gate, cobbled path with parapets, roses, glowing windows). For the Pale Spire use pale silver and violet instead of green, with red as the accent (this ties to the red skies of the Blighted Lands).
* ref12 (medieval courtyard): half-timbered upper storeys over stone arcades, a big stone arch bridging two buildings, a pitched-roof wellhouse, a clipped lawn beside a gravel path, red tile roofs. Take: variety in the facades (timber and stone), the bridging arch and gallery over the courtyard, the wellhouse, lawns beside paths.
* ref13 (garden stair and grotto): a curved stone stair with an ivy-covered balustrade, an arched grotto with a statue in it and a small stream into a lily pond, flower beds (red, orange, purple, white), a terrace with arches, a pigeon. Take: little garden alcoves with a statue in an arch, a stream and pond with lilies, flower beds in different colours, curved stairs. (In the Pale Spire the flowers are pale and wrong: white, bruise purple, one red.)
* ref14 (ivy cloister): a misty arcade with pointed arches covered in ivy, white statues on plinths inside the arches, mossy stone benches, wet cobbles, a balustrade with statues above, fog. Take: the nooks and alcoves of the courtyard and the cloister: statues on plinths in each arch, mossy benches, wet dark cobbles, fog, ivy veils.

Palette note: keep the Pale Spire cold (pale stone, silver, violet) with warm light only from windows and lamps, and red as the one accent (gate, roses, storm glow). The courtyard should feel strange and overgrown but tended, like a garden nobody is allowed to stop looking after.
