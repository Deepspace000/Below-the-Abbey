## What a schnoor is
The Schnoor is the oldest quarter of Bremen: a tangle of tiny, crooked, cobbled lanes, some barely one person wide, between narrow two and three storey half-timbered or brick gabled houses that lean and overhang (a jetty), with small leaded windows, little doors up a step or two, hanging signs, lanterns on brackets, flower boxes, and glimpses of a church spire between the roofs. In the castle: the Court of Statues becomes a schnoor of servants' and craftsmen's lanes inside the walls; arches and gates join the lanes; each lane has a view ending in something (a statue, a fountain, a door, a window, a bell).

## Rules for every map
* SMALLER AND TALLER. New sizes are fixed in src/desert/ps_ids.js (PSP.LEVELS and the new PSP.LINKS squares: THAT TABLE IS THE CONTRACT, do not change another level's squares; you may nudge your own door's approach inside your level). Walls tall (wall height `wh` 3 to 4.5 for halls, arches and tower walls; low 2.2 for lanes), high ceilings, outdoor lanes between walls taller than the player can see over.
* NO open flat floor. No room or yard may have an open rectangle bigger than about 6 by 6; break every larger space with columns, arcades, stairs, planters, fountains, statues in alcoves, iron fences, screens, half walls, trees, or divide it into separate rooms joined by arches. Lanes are 1 to 2 squares wide, rooms mostly 3 to 7 wide. Prefer winding routes, dead ends with a reward, loops, and shortcuts that open later.
* VERTICAL feeling: stairs up and down inside a level (use the floor height array `hgt` of blKit where it works, steps are floor-height changes), balconies and bridges the party walks over (a bridge is a one-square-wide span with a rail on each side and open air/water seen below), walk-under arches (walk-through arch decor `wd`, or wall-height changes), spiral stairs, towers, undercrofts with ribbed vaults, clock tower with a visible clock face and a mechanism room, bell rooms with big bells, large arched windows set high in walls throwing pale light down on the player.
* Every lane, room and hall needs at least three of: vines or ivy (hanging curtains and wall growth), moss, wrought iron (fence, gate, rail, lantern bracket), an alcove with a creepy statue, a lamp or candle pool of light, a painted window, a plinth or urn, a banner, a bell or clock, a doorway with a step. Each also needs one thing that makes it memorable (a view, a secret, a story prop). No two lanes alike.
* Light: outdoors an OVERCAST sky (grey heavy cloud, cold light, mist at the ground), pale lamps; indoors cold violet and silver with a few warm pools (candles) only in rest rooms and special places.
* Keep all gameplay content of the plan: the people, quests, items, creatures, mini bosses, the key and sacristy, the rest rooms, the waystone, links and locks. Re-place it inside the new geometry. Safe zones (waystone: none within 8 squares; rest rooms) are still rules.
* Everything must stay walkable and reachable: run the ps tests.

* Do not touch other packages' files, ps_ids.js squares, or index.html by hand. Report under 300 words: what is built, what the tests say, what is weak.
