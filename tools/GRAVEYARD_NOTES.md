## Done in the finishing pass, with what was changed
1. **Flag on, load and quest tests.** The quest test had two faults, both fixed. (a) The bell in Wenlock was lost whenever Wenlock had not been loaded yet: its loader calls `goToLevel` twice on a first visit and the second call wipes the timers, so `gyTownArrive` now only acts when `LEVEL===WL.ID`. This was a real bug in the game, not only in the test. (b) The test stood too far from the boss for the close-range moves; it now stands next to him.
2. **Mazes at the new wall height.** Creatures, bosses, props, rooms and torches looked right. Fixed: the plaque is two squares ahead of the arrival square (it was out of sight on the left; the candelabra moved to the other corner); the purple of the Pale Catacombs toned to lavender; no torch within 3 squares of the boss door, and a lower gate brightness per maze (the gate was washed out when seen from inside the boss room).
3. **Maps.** No captions under the title (names and key only); a name for every maze room (`own` table in `gy_mazes.js`: the Cross, Crown and Candle lever rooms, the Moot Hall, the Hall of Sorrow, the Rats' Nest, the Bone Hall); levels 531 to 535 use the dungeon pool of the painted marginalia (`marginalia_img.js`).
4. **Yard brightness and look.** `amb` .62 to .92 and `day` .66 in `gy_yard.js`, the mist lighter in colour and thicker (`weatherNow` in `gy_art.js`, haze .8, so the far end fades). The boundary wall is darker. The blank slab of Aldith's grave now has the grain of the painted grey stone texture (`gySlab`; it was a plain flat card). **One engine edit in `index.html`**: the ray loop `while(guard++<64)` is now `while(guard++<(LEVEL===530?100:64))`, because from the lych-gate the far wall of the 70-wide yard is 65 steps away, and the rays that ran out drew bright stripes at the horizon.
5. **Boss balance** (hp and `dmg` in `gy_art.js`, moves in `gy_boss.js`). Method: a script (not kept in the repo; easy to redo) that calls the real `heroAttack`, `heroSpecial`, `enemyAct` and `finishRound` as a real round does, with the party at the boss door in jump gear at level 84, 100 fights per boss, drinking at 38% health and healing at 55%. The first numbers gave a win rate of 36% (Aldric) and 60% (Morwenna) against 96% for Vane, so Aldric and Morwenna were cut and Vane and Grimsby raised a little. Now about **99%, 95%, 92% and 90%** wins in the script (harder than a person would find it in one way, because the script never wastes a turn; two fights through the real buttons were both won, with two heroes lost each time, in 20 and 30 rounds). A fight is about 15 rounds, the front hero drops to 450 to 700 health, the priest heals 7 to 10 times. Also the melee specials were being used from 3 squares away (the rapier lunge, the greatsword cleave); they now reach 2 and 1 squares. Things the fights showed that are not ours: a Healing Potion heals only 30 at level 84 (the priest's Heal, 1,000 to 1,700 for 3 mana, carries the party); the jump gear's spare weapons overload the party (hit chance down 10%), so drop them before a fight; heroes who are down get no experience.
6. **Wenlock side.** The lane (y=31, x 3 to 23) is walkable, the gate tile at (2,31) is the door to the graveyard, `tools/wl_walk.py` passes with the flag on (run a copy with port 8373: zero unreachable road cells, houses and people), and the gate and the way back were walked in the quest test.
7. **Saves.** Quit and reload in maze 2 (kills, a lever pulled, position), in the Black Barrow boss room (boss half dead, door open), in the crypt and in the yard after the ending, and in Wenlock: all identical after loading. Fixes: a loaded game inside the graveyard now gets the same arrival as walking in (a `restoreNow` wrapper in `gy_art.js`: the dressed grave, the lit shrine and the gone ghost were lost, because the pieces added on arrival are not part of a save); helpers called by a boss are no longer called twice after loading (the counter starts from the health before the blow), and everything but the boss in his room dies with him (a loaded game does not remember who was a helper).
8. **Full quest chain.** Passes (112 checks, 0 fails).
