# Below the Abbey: update notes, 3.779 to 3.838

The last update notes covered 3.769 to 3.778. These are the versions since (3.788 and 3.789 were never released). All of them are on GitHub Pages, and every release has also gone to itch.io (browser) and the Windows app.

## Yarrim and the Yarrim woods
- 3.779: Yarrim has roofs, drawn the same way as the roofs of Thornwick and Ironhill, so they sit on the houses and do not slide when you walk.
- 3.780: Yarrim's walls are more defined, and its roofs are a touch faded.
- 3.781: Yarrim has a round lighthouse.
- 3.782: green woods round Yarrim, with trails, wolves, bandits and chests.
- 3.784: the Pilgrim's Path comes out in the Yarrim woods.
- 3.785 and 3.786: the wolves and bandits stay in the woods, and there are fewer of them (5 wolves and 3 bandits). The lighthouse sign can be read from every angle.
- 3.787 and 3.811: bushes grow between the Yarrim woods and the beach.
- 3.812: the Yarrim donkey does not go to Oldhaven, and only goes to places of a lower level than Yarrim.
- 3.813: the Yarrim beach has painted shell, pebble, seaweed and stick pictures in many variants and far fewer pieces, so it no longer repeats one shell patch and one stick stack.

## Oldhaven
- 3.783: six new quests on the Oldhaven quay.
- 3.790: Oldhaven has roofs, and its quests are always added.
- 3.802: the six fair people tell the party how far along it is.
- 3.803: painted garden props in Oldhaven: a fountain, a rain barrel, a sundial and more.
- 3.804: a painted street lamp. Lamps and windows light up at night.
- 3.805: the castle grounds have two quests, pickups and three secret doors.
- 3.806: exactly one ordinary home in ten is infested.
- 3.814: Oldhaven strikes the hour on the quay and in the city, twelve times at noon and midnight.
- 3.815: Oldhaven Tales: ten new quests and three new dungeons under the city (the sewers, a crypt and a bandit hideaway).
- 3.816: the dungeons look different from each other: green mossy sewers, a reddish crypt, a darker torch-lit hideaway.
- 3.817: fewer rats in the sewers, and fanged green sewer slimes.

## Music and sound
- 3.791: new dungeon music. One of three pieces is chosen each time you enter.
- 3.792: a nature soundscape plays under the music in the open country.
- 3.793: a forest rain soundscape in the Hollow Grasslands and Blackbriar Wood.
- 3.794: lake rain on the Ironhill Road, following the rain.
- 3.795 to 3.801: a Credits page lists every piece of music and sound. The towns' music (Embers on the Flagstone) and the tomb and dungeon music are credited correctly. The old dungeon music plays in the desert dungeons, which now choose one of three pieces, and Golden Key joins them.

## Fighting and controls
- 3.807: higher level creatures hit more and take less damage. Cemetery music. Bloody flat bodies in infested houses. No blocked entrances in the catacombs and avenue vaults.
- 3.808: hold Space to keep attacking. The game and the manual say so.
- 3.809: every row of the Keyboard page fits.
- 3.810: a finger kept on the Attack button keeps attacking.
- 3.821: all creatures give 10 percent less XP.
- 3.824: critical hits do 1.3 times damage (it was 1.5 times).

## Weapons and gear
- 3.818: shop weapons for levels 62 to 78 (swords, axes, maces and bows, five tiers). The shops in Wenlock, Sale, the Deeper Hollow and Oldhaven sell every tier.
- 3.819: 32 painted shop weapons for levels 82 to 110, in eight sets, each with its own effects: burning, poison, curse, fear, blind, slow, chain lightning, a burst of force, and mana leech.
- 3.826: some armour sets and the top weapons carry extra critical chance and extra critical damage.
- 3.838: every piece of gear shows its level on its card, and its name and bag slot are coloured by level: grey for levels 0 to 4, green 5 to 9, blue 10 to 19, yellow 20 to 29, orange 30 to 39, red 40 to 59, purple 60 to 79, orange 80 to 99 and turquoise from 100 up. A piece with no level of its own gets one worked out from its price. The special named pieces keep a pink border on the bag slot. The pick-up message uses the same colour.
- 3.837: the weapons of levels 82 to 110 grow more gently from one level to the next. The Starseeker Bow (level 90) now does 351 to 505, where it did up to 590. The level 110 weapons top out around 615 to 660, where they reached about 950.

## Powers
The class powers were rebalanced several times. Where they stand now:
- 3.820: painted pictures for spell and power effects (shock rings, arrows, impacts, lightning, vines, spikes, chains, cracks, rays and more) replace the stiff drawn lines.
- 3.825 and 3.830: damage powers hit less hard, most at the top. The level 80 fighter, archer and rogue powers are a further 12 percent softer.
- 3.827 to 3.829: mage, sorcerer and priest powers hit much harder. The warrior, barbarian, ranger, archer, monk and goat powers were eased back. The Paladin's powers were raised (it still does double damage to skeletons), and the ranger and archer were raised about 11 percent.
- 3.834: powers hit softer as heroes level. Nothing changes below level 30, powers do about 87 percent of their old damage at level 50, 68 percent at level 80, and 55 percent from level 100 up.
- 3.835 and 3.836: the classes that already deal the most damage with their normal attacks have the weakest powers: the thief, assassin, archer, ranger and monk. A melee power swung with a bow in hand (a thief with an archer's bow) now hits half as hard.

## The Pale Spire, second and third floors
- 3.821 to 3.824: the illusion rooms look clearer. The Waterways has deep dark blue canals and lighter warm paving, so the water you cannot cross is obvious. The Nightmare has a bright chequered path with much darker ground beside it, and its trees and columns stand on black patches of ground. Fewer leaf-covered columns.
- 3.831: when all seven pieces are on her table the Sleeper wakes, in a cutscene. Her shadow tears loose and becomes the Sovereign's Dream, a boss with a second cutscene and three phases that call creatures out of the illusion rooms. It drops the Dreamweave Mantle (a level 100 chest piece, armour 88). When it is dead the Sleeper comes back and a swirling door opens where the iron door stood, leading to the third floor.
- 3.832: the third floor opens with the Hall of Mirrors. There is a nave lined with mirrors, with shades and hounds in it. In the Beam Room you turn three mirror posts so a beam of light reaches a crystal, which releases the Silver Seal on a reliquary with a chest. The Court of Glass has a maiden whose hand mirror leads to a hidden closet and a chest. There is also a quiet alcove to rest in. The stair to the Observatory Gallery is still shut.
- 3.833: a plainer line when you step through the maiden's mirror.
- 3.836: the leaf-covered columns are gone from the Nightmare. The Pale Spire now asks again for any picture that fails to load, instead of showing a grey stand-in column until the game is reloaded.

## Camp
- 3.831: "Cook a hearty meal" has eleven scenes instead of three, five different closing lines, and never shows the same scene twice in a row. The other camp activities no longer repeat the same scene twice in a row either.

## 3.839
- Pale Spire, third floor: the Observatory Gallery and the Sovereign's Reflection boss are open. The stair at the north end of the Hall of Mirrors opens once the crystal in the Beam Room is lit.

## 3.840
- Hall of Mirrors: the beam of light is now drawn, the four mirror posts look like mirrors, and the hidden closet has mirrors on its walls.

## 3.841
- Observatory Gallery: the five star charts are now five different objects (lamp, hound, throne, stair, mirror) and say plainly what the pins are for. Each chart has a "Read my notes" option that lists the clues you have found.

## 3.842
- Observatory Gallery redone as an arranging puzzle: a painted Chart of the Rising in the Gallery shows the five constellations in order; in the Chart Room you set the five star chart stands on five pedestals in that order. No more pins or clues to work out.
- Painted art for the Observatory: telescope, star chart stands, chart of the rising, orrery, star panel, mirror post (also used in the Beam Room), the Sovereign's Reflection, Mirror Shade, Star Wraith and Brass Clockwork Guardian.

## 3.843
- Attack pictures for the Sovereign's Reflection, Mirror Shade, Star Wraith and Brass Clockwork Guardian.

3.844: Observatory chart room pedestals redrawn small and straight on (empty pedestal plus one with each constellation disc); the pedestal menu now lists the constellations in a scrambled order.

3.845: top-down engine (src/desert/topdown.js) and the first planet, The Stone Planet (9891, src/desert/ps_f4_planets.js). Seen from above on a medieval map: parchment floors, inked stone walls, named rooms (Keep, Chapel, Herb Garden, Graveyard, Well Court, Guardroom), compass and border. Same grid, rounds and fights; arrows walk N/E/S/W, walking into a creature strikes it; only the lit squares in line of sight show, seen squares stay on the map. Reach the wax seal to finish the planet. Not yet reachable in play (the Floor of the Moon hub, 9890, is still a stub): jump with goToLevel(9891,{x:2,y:2,f:2}).
