How to read the numbers
  • Level: the level the creature has where it stands. An ordinary creature can be one level either side.
  • Type: Ordinary, Elite (set above the party's level) or Boss. A "+" marks a boss that is set above the party's level.
  • Damage: the range of one hit before armour (the raw number). Every creature attacks once a round.
  • Its hit lands (%): how often that attack lands on the party. It depends on the armour worn, so there is one figure for each gear set.
  • Damage per hit, after armour: what one landed hit takes from a hero. Armour takes a share off every blow: armour as good as the best the shops sell at the creature's level takes half off, less armour takes less off in proportion. Averaged over the party, front fighters counted three times as often as the back line.
  • Damage per round, on average: that hit times how often it lands. This is what one of these creatures costs the party each round.
  • Hits to kill it: health divided by one landed plain attack from an average hero of the party (critical hits counted, powers and spells not).
  • Rounds to kill it: health divided by what all four heroes do in a round with plain attacks, misses counted. Powers, spells and healing turns are not counted.
  • Gear (party of Warrior, Thief, Mage and Priest at the level of the area): Low = cheap and middling shop pieces, some slots empty, two good pieces on the Warrior. Med = a weapon about four fifths as good as the best in the shops, and armour about seven tenths as good. Best = the best shop gear of the level, no rare or reward pieces.
