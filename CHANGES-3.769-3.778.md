# Below the Abbey: update notes, 3.769 to 3.778

The last update on itch.io (browser and Windows) was 3.768. These are the ten versions since. All of them are already on GitHub Pages.

## People and quests
- 3.769: Sly Dunstan, Thornwick's fixer, has his own painted picture. He used to share the drunk's picture. He is now a shifty man in a cap and a patched jerkin, with a coin in one hand and a dagger hilt at his belt, drawn full length and straight on. Hob, the real drunk, is unchanged.

## Ironhill
- 3.770: Ironhill (level 13) has roofs. Every house has a pitched roof with chimneys. They use the same method as Thornwick's roofs: drawn through the same projection as the walls, so they sit exactly on the houses and do not slide or hang over the street when you walk.
  - The church has a tower, a spire and an iron cross.
  - The mayor's residence has a castle top: a crenellated parapet, four turrets with pointed spires and a keep.
  - Thornwick is unchanged.
- 3.771: Ironhill's house walls are lower, 2.0 high instead of the full outdoor height of 3.4. Before, the roofs were far above your view from the street and could not be seen. The forest round the town stays tall. The roofs use the same height.

## Menus
- 3.772: the journal and abilities menu now sizes its cards to the number of pages, so the last cards no longer run under the Close button. A summary that is too long for its card ends in dots. On the Camp and Bonds page, the last two lines no longer touch the Back button, and the right-hand column of bonds stays inside the frame.

## Rain
- 3.773: the thin line of splashing puddles along the bottom of the picture is gone, and so is the horizon spray that looked like puddles halfway up. What is left is the rain and some splash rings. Each splash is a real spot on the ground, projected every frame like the walls, so it moves with the ground as you walk. It no longer sits fixed on the screen. This applies to the Ironhill Road and every area that gets this rain.
- 3.774: about four times as many splash rings. They are placed in front of the party, most of them close. They are large near you and small far away, and each is still a real spot on the ground. There are still no puddles and no line.

## Desert
- 3.775: rocks and trees on the dunes stand on the ground from every side. On the Sand Road and the other real dunes, a rock or a tree was set at the ground height under three points and drawn with a flat bottom, so on a steep dune one side hung in the air or looked buried, depending on the direction you looked from. Now each stands on the lowest ground under its whole width (five points across), and anything below the surface of the dune at its distance is hidden column by column. So it is planted from every side.
- 3.776: Sut, Lord of the Red Desert, is level 41. He was level 40, only one above the Tomb of Sut. He is now two levels above it.
- 3.777 and 3.778: the Red Dunes are barer, more like open dune desert. Dead trees 113 down to 23, rocks 198 down to 77, plants 76 down to 40, and about a third as many shrubs. The dunes, ruins, obelisks and creatures are unchanged.
