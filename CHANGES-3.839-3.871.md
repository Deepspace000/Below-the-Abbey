# Below the Abbey: update notes, 3.839 to 3.871

The last update notes covered 3.779 to 3.838. These are the versions since (3.855, 3.856, 3.858 and 3.862 were never released). All of them are on GitHub Pages, and the latest, 3.871, is also on itch.io (browser and Windows app).

## The Pale Spire: the Observatory and the Hall of Mirrors (floor 3)
- 3.839: the third floor of the Pale Spire opens, with the Observatory Gallery and the Sovereign's Reflection.
- 3.840: the beam in the Hall of Mirrors is drawn, with mirror posts and closet mirrors.
- 3.841: the Observatory puzzle is clearer, there is a notes option, and each chart looks different.
- 3.842: the Observatory arranging puzzle, with painted props and a painted boss.
- 3.843: attack pictures for the Sovereign's Reflection, the Mirror Shade, the Star Wraith and the Brass Clockwork Guardian.
- 3.844: the pedestals in the chart room are small and straight on, and the pedestal menu lists the constellations in a scrambled order.

## The Floor of the Moon (floor 4)
- 3.845: a new way of seeing a maze: from above, like a medieval map, with parchment floors, inked walls and named rooms. The first planet, the Stone Planet, is built this way.
- 3.846: before the first planet maze the party talks about being drawn from above.
- 3.847: the Floor of the Moon is built: a Moon Hall with four planet rooms (Stone, Ice, Growing and Fire, each with its own look, rooms and creatures), an iron gate, and the Pale Moon boss.
- 3.848: no leaf-covered columns on the Floor of the Moon, the planets or floor 3. They are plain fluted columns.
- 3.849: planet exits glow gold with an EXIT arrow, and a little planet charm (a bag bonus) lies at the end of each planet maze.
- 3.850 and 3.851: new wording on the iron gate, and it opens when the four little planets are in the bag.
- 3.853: the planet exit and the little planet are far apart, and planet progress counts the little planets you carry.

## The Ramparts (floor 5)
- 3.854: floor 5 is built: a night walk along the castle ramparts with a thin black-green Pale Tower in the middle that you can walk all the way round, on a dais, with a thin fenced stair up to it.
- 3.857: far lit towns on the horizon, and the dais wall is flush so the view over it is clear.
- 3.859: red candelabras and gothic lampstands round the spire, and a finer stair.
- 3.860 and 3.861: plain painted columns on the Moon floor, steadier lights, a gentler stair, and no side walls on the Ramparts stair.

## The Pale Tower and the Pale King (floor 6)
- 3.863: the Pale Tower: ten small boss rooms and the Pale King's throne room. A rule is added so that leaf-capital columns can never appear.
- 3.864: all new art for the tower: baroque gothic walls, floors and furnishings, and ten bosses plus the Pale King.
- 3.865: the room bosses are weaker, the Pale King has a confrontation scene, and there are ending slides.
- 3.866: a Pale King quest, round tower rooms, chests that open in a loot window, new gear icons and chest art, a quest audit, and balance and performance fixes.

## The late game
- 3.867: houses cost more, six super weapons are sold by Ysolde Vane, the Last Smith, in the Watch Nook on the ramparts (about 100,000 gold each), a bag that overflows no longer loses items, and late quest gold is halved.
- 3.868 and later: super weapons have a cream white name, above turquoise.
- 3.869: New Game Plus after the Pale King, as an end game option.

## Balance and gear (3.870)
- The weapon ladder is filled in. There are 18 daggers and 18 wands, one every four levels from 44 to 112, each with its own painted picture and its own effects. There are new swords and bows at levels 42, 46, 54 and 58, two new axes, five new maces, and a smooth power curve for swords and bows from level 50 up. Before, a level 62 sword was weaker than the level 50 one.
- Ordinary creatures from level 30 up hit harder, area by area, so a party with ordinary gear loses about a third to two fifths of its health in a fight. A party with the best gear loses only about a fifth, so top gear still feels very strong. The spikes in a few areas (levels 72, 74, 100, 107 and 108) are eased. Bosses are not changed.
- An XP gate: a party that is above an area's level gets much less experience there, and no single reward gives more than two levels. An area now lifts the party by about one level, so the next one is not trivial.
- The Testing menu has parties for levels 105, 110 and 115.

## More to find and spend on (3.871)
- Ten new armour sets (helm, chest, arms and legs), one between each of the old ones, at levels 15, 25, 35, 45, 55, 75, 85, 95, 105 and 110, and eleven new shields up to level 110. All 51 pictures are painted.
- The Last Smith will temper the gear you wear, one step at a time up to +10. Each step adds 3 percent to a weapon's damage or a piece of armour's armour, and costs more than the last. It is a place to spend gold after the main story.
- Eight new optional dungeons with quests, at levels 22, 52, 54, 92, 94, 96 and 98: the Old Quarry, the Weeping Well, the Smugglers' Hold, the Bellfounders' Cellar, the Sexton's Burrow, the Ringers' Undercroft, the Mill Race and the Toll Vaults. Each is on the world map and has a quest giver in a nearby town. The creatures come back each time you go in, so they are good for levelling and gold.
- The Pale King hits much less hard. He used to kill a hero in one blow. Now he hits for 400 to 600 and has 140,000 health.
- The ghost, the hunter and the hitman now have painted pictures, with attack pictures, in place of the old drawn ones.
- The red candelabras and lamps on the Ramparts use their own paintings. They were showing as the ivy column. In the whole Pale Spire, a picture that has not loaded yet now shows nothing, never the ivy column, and the pictures start loading as soon as any Pale Spire level is built.
- The world map now opens zoomed in on the area you are in. Zoom out (down to 30 percent) to see how big the world is.
- A balance report with the checks behind all of this is in the project (`Below-the-Abbey-balance-report-3.871.pdf`).
