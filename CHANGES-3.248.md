# Below the Abbey: update 3.248

Everything new since update 3.206.

## The Orange Spire (the old Orange Tower)

- **Renamed.** The tower in the Hollow Land is now the Orange Spire, on every map, journal page and quest.
- **It is on the world map**, drawn as an orange castle with its level range, west of the Abbey, where the Hollow Land will grow. Its local map has a title banner with the floor, such as "The Orange Spire, level 6".
- **Four rulers, four relic weapons.** The Gaunt King, the Smiling Queen, the Poison Duke and the fourth ruler each hold the last hall of levels 4, 9, 14 and 19. Each drops a twisted, very powerful weapon with a painted icon. Hidden rooms hold good, moderate weapons.
- **Sixteen new horrors**, one or two added every second level from level 3: mad jesters, chest mimics, shape shifters, ghosts, wights, phantoms, headless knights, butchers, the insane, the undead, vampires, werewolves, abominations, murder victims, demented dukes and banshees. Creatures stay small enough to fit the window.
- **Trap rooms** on every third level. The doors vanish, the party searches for a switch, a laugh starts, the lights die, a red light rises, the walls fall away into a black plane and a horror appears. Kill it and the room returns. The Hideous Phantom drops the Phantom Shroud.
- **The bogeyman.** He shows in the glass of mirrors, then attacks at the party's own level. Each mirror does it once. He drops the Bogeyman Mask, a helm that shields the wearer from fear and haunting.
- **Secret rooms and hidden doors.** Walls of the wrong colour open with an old wooden lever. Some bookshelves open when you pull the red book. Plain-looking hidden doors open when you walk into them and show on the map.
- **A hand-drawn castle map** with a legend. Room names are scrambled until you have been inside.
- **Dressing.** Painted stone plaques with the level number, claw marks, more portraits and mirrors, more red torches in the narrow halls, darker halls, and tables kept out of the one-square passages.
- **Strange food drops here.** Mystery Meat, Eyeball Lychees (they are eyeballs), Goblin Tongues, Bogeyman Sausage, Ghostly Sushi, Pickled Toes, Eyeball Soup and Fried Bat Wings. They look dreadful, but they are ordinary food that fills hunger and heals a little.
- **More potions drop here**, and they are strong ones.

## The Hollow Court story

- The Orrery of Hours is now explained in the quests and the journal.
- Brother Sevrin asks the party to find three letters Vance wrote, hidden in the secret rooms on levels 5, 10 and 15.
- At the top, the wizard tells who Vance really served.
- Three new journal chapters.

## Difficulty and levels

- **A fixed world by default.** Each place has its own level and each place on the ladder is three levels above the last. The world map shows the level range of each place.
- **Autoscaling is optional in the Gameplay menu.** Turning it on now rescales every creature, not just some.
- **Potions fit the level.** Healing potions that drop follow the level of the party and the place. Past level 60 there are no basic healing potions. Three new top tiers: Supreme (900 health), Mythic (1800) and Divine (3500).

## Menus and controls

- **A ready-made party** to choose when you start a new game, or a random one.
- **Controller support**, with a Controller page that shows the pad and what each button does.
- **A tidier main menu.** Exit Game is at the bottom and the less used buttons are tucked into other menus.
- **F5 quicksaves** instead of reloading the page.
- **The hunger bar** stays visible in the inventory, under the portraits and on the right.
- **Enemy name plate** clears the quicksave disc and no longer runs into the level.
- **Resting:** choosing an activity no longer leaves the Rest button dead for a long time, and the camp story scene shows up again.

## Other changes

- A little more light: more wall torches in every dark level, most of all in narrow passages.
- The Wailing Ghost is lowered so its face can be seen in battle.
- Saint Elen's Monastery and Yarrim are placed on land on the world map.
- Church and mausoleum roofs at the monastery now match their buildings.
- Travelling by teleport stone plays a soft rising swell instead of the level-up arpeggio.
- Rough playing time for the main story: 15 to 20 hours.
