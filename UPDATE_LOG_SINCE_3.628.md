# Below the Abbey: the road west (what's new since 3.628)

Versions 3.629 to 3.653. Most of this is the first stretch of the western lands. The Western Kingdoms themselves are not open yet: the road west out of Wenlock is a shut gate. Everything before it is in.

## The way there, in order

**1. The gate in the Deeper Hollow (3.640)**
- Aldous Wend stands at a new tall gate in the north wall of the great atrium and gives the quest "The Gate in the Atrium" (3,000 gold, 20,000 xp, a potion). The gate leads out of the Hollow Lands.
- On the way, Cinder gives "The Black Swill" (see below).

**2. The Maze of Gardens (level 490, 3.640)**
- A sunny hedge maze, 91 by 91 squares, about 760 steps from the south gate to the north gate, with 19 rooms cut out of it.
- Fountains, statues, lamps, plaques, peacocks, ducks, rabbits, bees, birdsong and chests.
- Guards wake when you get close (a warning shows at 5 squares): knights, hounds, gargoyles, lions, briar beasts, snapping plants and the Hedge Warden.
- It has its own map with a numbered list of rooms. The lych-gate at the far end leads to Wenlock.

**3. Wenlock (level 496, 3.640)**
- A country market town, 100 by 100: a church with a tower, a village hall, a windmill, a watermill, a well, a green with a pond, an orchard, a brook, a market cross, stocks and a maypole.
- About 45 people and animals walk about. It has wind, birdsong, an hour bell and a smith's hammer.
- Every door opens to a room:
  - Shops with level 80 stock (potions up to potion 7, mana, escape, and the best arms and armour under 30,000 gold).
  - An inn (room 60 gold, a meal, gossip) with a cellar tunnel to an old granary.
  - A church where the vicar heals, raises the dead and removes curses.
  - A school, an almshouse, a library with eight books to read, and votive candles (5 gold for a blessing).
  - Homes furnished in 14 different ways, with pets and children. A ghost named Percival lives in the town.
- Walking in finishes the quest "wk2" (4,000 gold, 25,000 xp). Speaking to the mayor finishes "wk3" (6,000 gold, 40,000 xp).
- The mayor then gives a chain of ten quests, passed between the crier, Goody Prue, Mrs Tanner, Constable Fudge, Jack Joiner, Curate Pettigrew, Mrs Suds and Sexton Moss. Quest 8 opens the town well and gives the Belfry Key.

**4. The Purple Maze under Wenlock's well (3.640, ratings changed in 3.650)**
- Four levels of purple brick: Under the Wenlock Well (rated 80), The Violet Maze (81), The Bell Vaults (82) and The Amethyst Deep (83).
- Each stair door needs its own key. The Violet Key is in a mimic chest in the Collapsed Mine. The Amethyst Key is behind a riddle door (the answer is "a well"). The Warden's Key is in a secret closet at a dead end.
- One puzzle per level: a scale that must read exactly 80, a mirror hall with left and right swapped, a bell code (low LONG, middle SHORT, high LONG) and lanterns to light in order (lilac, gold, violet).
- Also levers, cranks, hidden pit slabs, a rowing boat in the cistern, and secret closets.
- The boss, the Well-Warden (about 30,900 health at level 80), sits at the top. Reward: the Warden's Amethyst Edge (a level 80 sword), gold, 48,000 xp and potions. It stays dead.

**5. The Witch's Wood (level 520, rated 82, 3.650)**
- A dark foggy forest north of Wenlock, reached by Blackbriar Lane to an iron gate. Ten glades, a dried brook, scarecrows, hounds, spiders and crows, and its own map.
- The witch's cottage has three more levels: the parlour (521), the cellar (522) and the kitchen with the great oven (523).
- Eight quests called "Into the Oven" (about 83,600 xp in all) lead to a fight with the witch Grisselda Blackbriar, level 82, about 31,000 health. She flees at half health and is cornered at 12 percent.
- Rewards include the Hag-Stone Ring and the Singed Hat. A gingerbread stall opens in Wenlock's square and some townsfolk have new lines.

**6. The Black Swill (3.640)**
- A level 80 boss on the big island in the Underlevel's black sea. Bring a boat, healing, mana and escape potions. It is rated Deadly (about 55 rounds), splits into slimes as it is hurt, and does not come back once dead.
- Reward: 20,000 gold, 100,000 xp, the Swillcleaver and potions. It also drops the Swillhide Mantle.

**7. The Wenlock Graveyard (levels 84 to 87, 3.653)**
- Now open. A lane at the end of Churchyard Lane in Wenlock leads through a lych-gate to the graveyard (level 530, rated 84): a gate path, a plaza, a ruined chapel, a family plot, a gravedigger's hut and 55 readable epitaphs, under yews, ravens, lanterns and dusk mist. Lady Aldith's Crypt (531) is below.
- Four torch-lit mazes, each with its own colour of torchlight and a boss: The Red Ossuary (84, Lord Corwin Vane), The Grey Charnel (85, Digger Grimsby), The Black Barrow (86, Sir Aldric the Forsworn) and The Pale Catacombs (87, Prioress Morwenna). Each boss has special moves and calls helpers at two thirds and one third health, and drops a relic.
- Five ghost quests lead to Lady Aldith's ending: a ring and a key, a name-stone and levers, a locket and a hunt for eight spirits, a book and a trapped chest, and the grave itself. Rewards add up to about 70,000 xp and 42,000 gold, with a lantern, a sword, a ring and a rose. After the ending the crypt becomes a lit shrine, and a bell rings in Wenlock.
- Every maze room has a name on its map. The graveyard map has no captions.
- Boss fights were tuned by 100 scripted fights each: now about 90 to 99 percent winnable by a level 84 party, about 15 rounds. Two real fights were won, losing two heroes each. Boss gold is high (15,000 to 23,000 each).

**Better gear in the early shops (3.653)**
- Thornwick and Ironhill shops now sell higher tiers (green, then blue to red, with the odd purple piece in Ironhill) up to five levels above your party level: Aldous's Store, the Witch's Shelf, Brannoch's Forge, Dorn's Store, Steelhold Blades, Ironhill Plate and Mail, and The Cauldron.
- Also new: Fenwick's Goods in Anaea (red and purple gear), the Hunter's Pack in Gandrick's Wood, and the Peddler's Cart in the crypt. The abbey grounds sell nothing.
- New items: 8 daggers and 8 staves (levels 1 to 30), a Morning Star, an Iron-Rimmed Shield, Chain Sleeves and Leggings, and the Wraith Knife, Kingsbane, Dawnwood Staff and Archmage's Staff (levels 35 and 40). Stock is one of each.
- Fixed: a shop's stock was not saved, so loading a game restocked it and brought back bought-out items. Fixed: trees stood on the doors of Corvin's Arcana and the Cauldron, so you could not walk in.
- Checked by script: every class has usable gear in every shop at the levels tested. 266 buy and equip checks passed and selling pays the listed price.

**Sut is tougher (3.653)**
- Sut, Lord of the Red Desert, is now level 40, one above the Tomb of Sut's area level of 39. He had been level 39.

## The world map and the atlas (3.650)
- The western lands are now on the world map, with the roads between them: Garden Road, Blackbriar Lane, Churchyard Lane, the road west, and Well Lane.
- Marks for Wenlock, the gardens, the Witch's Wood, the Purple Maze, Under the Well, the Underlevel and a shut Western Kingdoms gate. Wenlock was moved west to make room.
- New **Atlas** button: four pages listing every maze, dungeon and hidden place, with levels and ratings. A gold dot shows where you have been.
- New marks for the 52 desert dungeons and the Sand Road burrows. Names show when you zoom in.

## Also in these versions
- **Screen page (3.629):** full screen, picture shape (fit, fill or whole pixels), smooth or hard-edged pixels.
- **Keyboard page (3.629):** every key can be changed. **Controller page (3.630):** buttons can be changed too.
- **Enemy panel (3.630):** a small - button shrinks it to the fight rating, rounds to win and the gauge.
- **Fonts (3.629)** are now part of the game, so text looks the same everywhere. The Deeper Hollow map labels no longer overlap.
- **3.631:** the game asks for keyboard focus when it loads, and says "Click the game once to use the keyboard" if it has none. Before this, W A S D did nothing in the itch.io page until you clicked.
- **3.641:** no teleporting while a fight is on.
- **3.651:** bandits ask for mercy less often (about 1 in 8), and a bandit you strike after he surrenders fights on.
- **3.650:** the Go to level "+" button now goes up to 88.

## Not live yet
- **The Western Kingdoms:** the road west of Wenlock stays shut.
- **Still to do for the shops:** the new gear uses recoloured plain icons, not painted art. The Gandrick's Wood hunter was only tested by moving the party next to him. No balance playtest with the shops on.

## Not tested
- Going back up the ladder from the Purple Maze to Wenlock was only tested as far as the function call.
- A save made in the middle of the Black Swill fight loses the boss's health.
- Saves made inside a Wenlock room before 3.650: the room numbers changed, and there is no note on whether old saves still work.
- The itch.io browser build and the iPad have not been tested with the graveyard and shops. Some graveyard art is rough: the Bone Hound looks like a thin man and the yews look plum in the mist.
- The automated playtest only reached Act 1, step 7. Acts 2 to 8 have not been played.
