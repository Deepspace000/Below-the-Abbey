# Below the Abbey: update 3.280

Everything new since update 3.248.

## Armour

- **Ten full armour sets for levels 10 to 100**, each with a helm, chest, arms and legs, and a painted picture for every piece: Boarhide, Ironscale, Rimeforged, Emberplate, Sunsteel, Voidglass, Stormwrought, Wyrmbone, Starforged and Godforged.
- **New armour skills.** Ward cuts every blow that lands on you by a set amount. Life drain heals you for a share of the melee damage you deal. Second wind heals you at once, once every 30 rounds, when you fall under a quarter health. Armour also gives dodging, regeneration, thorns, extra damage, double hits, faster mana and slower hunger.
- **Set bonuses.** Wear all four pieces of a set and its bonus wakes.
- **Where to find it.** The armouries stock the sets up to ten levels above the party. Chests drop pieces from the best sets the party or the place has reached.

## Saint Elen's Monastery and the Pilgrim's Path

- **The Land Path is now the Pilgrim's Path**, on every map, journal page and quest.
- **Painted fronts on every monastery building.** Stained glass, banners, a rose window, ivy and roses, shuttered windows with flower boxes, saints in niches, and flowerpots at the doors.
- **The Chapel of the Lamps.** A new chapel east of the church, with an ornate spire and turrets. Inside there is a tall ceiling, a rose window and coloured glass down both walls, an aisle of little pews, an altar with two golden angels and two marble saints, candelabra, a lectern, a font, and chandeliers. Sister Agnes keeps the lamps.
- **Saint Elen's on the world map** now stands on the northern land, halfway along the long route between Yarrim and the Pilgrim's Path.

## The Orange Spire

- **The levels shrink much faster.** Level 10 is half the width and height of level 1, and the top levels are tiny, down to a small two-room hall on level 20.
- **Levels 58 to 65.** Its creatures now run from level 58 on the first floor to 65 on the top.

## Money and levels

- **Gold grows with the creature's level**, so the party can afford better weapons as it climbs.
- **Creatures above your level pay more.** One level above pays half as much again (+50%), and each level beyond adds a fifth more.
- **Humanoid creatures always drop gold.** That covers bandits, guards, goblins, orcs, skeletons, vampires and the like.
- **Quest rewards are doubled**, both gold and XP.
- **Ironhill:** a chest with 1000 gold hides in the dead end behind the castle. Iron ore sells for 50 gold a chunk in any shop, and the mines hold twice as much of it.
- **New levels for the later places.** The Burial Ground is level 29 and Ironhill Moor is 30. The Sand Road is 36, the Tomb of Sut 39, the Red Dunes 41, Khemetra 43 to 45, the other deserts 45, Yarrim 48, the islands and the sea 49 to 50, Oldhaven 55, and the floors below the Abbey 56 to 58.
- **Choose how the world levels when you start a new game.** Traditional means every place has its own level, and sometimes you must come back later, stronger. Scaled means creatures grow with your party, a smoother and slightly easier game for the casual player. You can change it at any time in Menu, Settings, Gameplay.

## Screens and menus

- **A loading screen** with a progress bar. If something fails to load, it shows what went wrong.
- **The title screen** now has Settings and Exit to desktop.
- **The save and load screen scrolls**, and its text no longer runs behind the buttons.
- **The Controller page** shows a broader pad with every button on it.

## World map

- **Level numbers sit beside the area names**, and labels slide out of each other's way so nothing is hidden.
- **The desert places are spread out** to give the names more room.
- **Roads** are drawn over land only, plus one route over the sea to Yarrim. The other dotted lines are gone.
