## Where the looks are weakest (critic and my own read)
1. **Heavy-hit powers** (Warrior, Barbarian, Goat, Monk big ones): six to eight different powers all show the same impact burst.
2. **Dark blade powers** (Thief and Assassin): about nine powers show a similar dark streak.
3. **Party buffs** (Shield Wall, Divine Shield, Mage Armor, Blessing, Hunter's Mark, Vanish): the same few glows on the portraits.
4. **Heal and Siphon**: small and faint; no life-drain picture.
5. **Still stiff** (critic): net, speed lines, holy flare cross, light-ray burst, magic circle, clock, spin.
6. **No pictures at all for**: poison, curse, drain, sleep, fear, summon, bleed, wind, shields, necrotic.
