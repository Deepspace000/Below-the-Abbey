# 12 Nov update: BALANCE, SPEED, WELL FIXES and SAVE BACKUP (3.895 to 3.904)

## Fights
- A balance pass from the very start to the Pale King.
- Ordinary creatures now last about three rounds, with a difficulty band that works both ways.
- Armour takes a share off every blow.
- Seven armour sets heal you less on their own.

## Speed and safety
- The 3D view is several times faster.
- The Pale Spire's pictures are put away after the party leaves, so the game stays light.
- A safety net now protects the game loop and your saves.
- Test visits keep their own saves and never touch yours.

## World map
- The world map has the curved coast, as on the itch.io page.

## Windows app and controller
- The attack button on a controller repeats when held.
- The Windows app shows a banner when an update is downloading or ready, with a Restart now button.
- The loading screen says Checking for updates, then up to date, downloading or ready.

## Thornwick well
- The well now stands beside the inn, off Market Street, and is no longer in the middle of the road. You walk up to it and ask, you can't stumble onto it.
- Climbing back up puts you on the square you went down from, facing away from the well.
- In the tunnels you start facing the open passage, not a wall.
- The Under the Well map now marks the way out at the stair. Before, the marker sat on a wall in the corner.
- Quest rings for Gandrick no longer show in the tunnels.

## Quests
- The quest log shows every quest's whole text. Long quests were cut off after two lines.

## Windows app: saves are backed up
- The Windows app now keeps a second copy of your saves in Documents\Below the Abbey\Saves (saves.json, plus saves.prev.json and saves.start.json from earlier moments).
- If the app ever starts with no saves in its own storage, for example after an update, it puts them back from that copy before the title screen.
- Erasing a slot in the game erases it from the copy too, and test visits are never copied.
- This needs the new Windows app (3.904 or later). Update through the itch app or the installer, then play once so the copy is made.
