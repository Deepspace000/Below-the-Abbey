# The main quest line of Below the Abbey (as of v3.631)

Read from the game itself: the 63 chapters of the Story So Far page (src/desert/story.js), the "what to do next" chain in storyNext(), and the quest tables. "Level" is the level rating of the place, which is the level of the creatures there. Steps 1, 2, 4 and 11 to 13 are story moments with no quest in the log. Rewards are gold and experience.

## Branches that belong to the story

- **The Singing Shell (shell)**: Brother Aldous, the Isle of Bells (49). Dive all four reefs and bring him the four pieces of the shell. The reward is the Nautilus Helm (665 gold, 15,000 xp).
- **Saint Elen's Monastery** (on the Pilgrim's Path, 55): the monastery quests (mq1 to mq10) and the Old Shrines are a side area in the middle of the Pilgrim's Path.
- **The Hollow Grasslands** (66 to 70): reached through the portal at the top of the sealed Orange Spire. It has its own quests (the hgq, hgb, dq, fw, wq and bq series) and is not part of the main chain so far.

## What is still open

The road after Wenlock (the western kingdoms) is the next part of the game, not in the game yet.

The latest main chapters before Act 7 (60 to 63) are the Violet Tower, the Hollow Court, Vance's Letters and the Wizard's Answer. The letters, the Pale Sovereign and the dead court under the Abbey set up the next part of the game, which is not in the game yet.
